# CROSS-CARD CONSISTENCY DECISIONS (apply on every card)

1. **Gonioscopy grading = Aravind 4.2's tables (ARAVIND fitz 216–217).** Never the typed format's degree ranges.
   - Shaffer: grade 4 wide open 35–40° (up to ciliary body; closure impossible) · grade 3 open 25–35° (up to ciliary
     body; closure impossible) · grade 2 moderately narrow 20° (trabecular meshwork, pigmented; closure possible but
     unlikely) · grade 1 extremely narrow 10° (only Schwalbe's line, maybe anterior trabecular meshwork; closure
     possible) · slit angle < 10° · grade 0 closed.
   - Scheie: wide open (all structures visible) · grade 1 narrow (hard to see the root of iris) · grade 2 narrow (ciliary
     body band obscured) · grade 3 narrow (posterior trabecular meshwork obscured) · grade 4 narrow (only Schwalbe's line
     visible).
   - Spaeth: FOUR parameters — iris root insertion A–E · angle width 10–40° · peripheral iris contour S/R/Q · trabecular
     meshwork pigmentation 0–4.
   - Occludable angle (Aravind): pigmented trabecular meshwork not visible (Shaffer grade 1 or 0) without indentation in
     at least three quadrants.
2. **Gonioscopy cross diagrams (`:::gonio`) on every card: write the DEEPEST STRUCTURE SEEN in each quadrant** — SS
   (scleral spur), CBB (ciliary body band), TM (trabecular meshwork), SL (Schwalbe's line) or "Closed" — exactly as on
   last year's exam sheet. Do NOT pair a Roman-numeral grade with a structure inside the diagram (the books do not define
   the department's "modified Shaffer" pairing). The caption names the conclusion, e.g. "Deepest structure seen in each
   quadrant — open angles in all quadrants". Card G9 carries one line: "Your department writes a grade in each quadrant
   'by modified Shaffer's grading' (III in all four on its POAG sheet); confirm with your seniors which structure each
   grade stands for, and write the structure too."
3. **Van Herick**: grade 4 — peripheral anterior chamber depth ≥ 1 corneal thickness (the department slide: 1:1 =
   grade 4) · grade 3 — ¼ to ½ corneal thickness · grade 2 — ¼ · grade 1 — < ¼. Peripheral depth < ¼ corneal thickness
   → angle may be occludable (Baidya, Namrata).
4. **Bjerrum's area**: 10–25° from fixation (Baidya, the newer book) on every card.
5. **Goldmann applanation mires**: green (Baidya).
6. **ISGEO**: write "the ISGEO classification" and do NOT expand the letters (the books print a garbled expansion).
7. **Digits** are allowed inside say-it scripts (6/18, 0.8). Abbreviations are not (write "millimetres of mercury",
   "cup–disc ratio", "right eye").
8. **Kanski fallback** (only where all three books lack the topic) must carry the inline tag
   *(Kanski — not in your practical books)*. Approved uses: G4 lens extraction / phacoemulsification in angle closure,
   the EAGLE study and the ZAP trial (KANSKI fitz 381 = printed p.377); G7 the definition of hemiretinal
   (hemispheric / hemicentral) vein occlusion (KANSKI fitz 525 = printed p.521). Add "Kanski p.377" / "Kanski p.521" to
   that card's @readmore line.
9. **"Cidamex"** in the NVG mnemonic: keep the mnemonic word as printed and gloss it once as "(oral acetazolamide)".
10. **No book names or page numbers in the card body**; they go only in @readmore (printed pages: Baidya = fitz − 14,
    Namrata = fitz − 18, Kanski = fitz − 4; Aravind by section number).
11. **Central corneal thickness (CCT) wording** (8 Oct 2026, from G8): Goldmann applanation is calibrated for a mean CCT
    of **520 µm**; a thinner cornea reads falsely low and a thicker one falsely high, by about **0.7 mm Hg per 10 µm**
    (Aravind 4.1, fitz 210; Baidya fitz 754). Categories: thin **< 500 µm**, thick **> 570 µm** (Namrata fitz 196).
    Use the same wording on G1, G8 and every card that mentions CCT.
