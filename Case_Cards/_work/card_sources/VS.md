## How this atlas was made
- A screenshot was taken **every 10 seconds** through the whole recording, plus extra frames at every scene change, so that slides shown for only a second or two were not missed.
- Identical screenshots were merged into one slide; webcam views were set aside.
- Each slide is listed with a short summary of its content and of what was said while it was on screen.
- **During the vivas no slides were shared** except the MRI in Case 3 and the enlarged anterior-segment photo in Case 7; the rest of each viva is webcam only.
