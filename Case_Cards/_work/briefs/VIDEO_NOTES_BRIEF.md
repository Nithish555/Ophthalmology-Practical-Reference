Source: YouTube live session "Ophthalmology PG Long case Live — Survival tips explained", Cochin Ophthalmic Club,
7 March 2021, 4 h 05 min. Postgraduate candidates present long cases to senior examiners, who interrupt, criticise and
ask viva questions; then the examiners give survival tips. Only the auto-generated captions are available (no slides,
no video frames: YouTube blocked the download). The reader is a final-year M.S. Ophthalmology candidate (TNMGRMU, exam
16 Oct 2026) whose weak areas are case presentation, fundus description and retinoscopy.

Your input: one part of the timed transcript, `Case_Cards/_work/cache/video/partN.txt` (repo root
`/home/user/Ophthalmology-Practical-Reference`). Lines are `[mm:ss] text`; `=== CHAPTER … ===` lines mark YouTube's
auto-chapters (the chapter names are automatic and often wrong — e.g. "Pina", "REM" are garbled names; work out what
each segment really is from the speech).

Auto-captions garble medical terms (e.g. "coachin ofi club" = Cochin Ophthalmic Club). Correct obvious ones from
context; when unsure, write your best reading followed by [?]. Never invent content that is not in the transcript.

## Write `Case_Cards/_work/cache/video/notes_partN.md` with these sections

1. **Timed summary** — for each real segment (not just the auto-chapters): `[start–end] Title` then 3–8 bullets in your
   own words: what happened, what was presented, what was criticised.
2. **The case(s)** — for each long case in this part: the patient summary as presented (age/sex/complaint), the
   candidate's diagnosis, the examiners' corrected or preferred diagnosis line, and **what the examiners criticised**
   in the presentation (order, wording, missing history points, examination omissions, wrong terms), each with its
   timestamp.
3. **Survival tips** — every piece of exam advice given (how to present, how to behave, what to say/not say, time
   management, how to handle not knowing), as numbered one-line tips with the timestamp.
4. **Viva questions and expected answers** — every question an examiner asked, the candidate's answer in brief, and
   **the answer the examiners expected or gave** (if the examiners corrected the candidate, give the correction). Format:
   `Q [mm:ss]: …` / `Expected: …` / (optional) `Candidate said: …`. If the examiners never gave the answer, write
   "Expected: not stated in the session" — do NOT supply one from your own knowledge.
5. **Slides and images mentioned** — wherever a speaker refers to a slide, photo, field chart or image ("as you can see
   here…"), note the timestamp and what they say it shows. (We cannot see the slides.)
6. **Garbled terms** — a short list of the caption errors you corrected and anything you marked [?].

  Do not copy long stretches of the transcript.
- No patient-identifying details beyond age and sex. Candidates' and examiners' names: omit them (write "the
  candidate", "the examiner").
- Be complete on sections 3 and 4 — they are the most valuable. Be concise elsewhere.
- Reply with 3 lines: the cases in your part (topic + timestamps), the number of tips, the number of viva questions.
