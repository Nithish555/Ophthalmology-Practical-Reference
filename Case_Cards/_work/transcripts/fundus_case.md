# FUNDUS CASE — transcription of PDF pages 1–20 (anonymised)

- **Source file:** `FUNDUS CASE.pdf` (uploads › Books › Practicals Reference; linked locally as `/home/claude/cards/src/fundus_case.pdf`). Read from the page photos `/home/claude/cards/img/fundus_case/p01.jpg`–`p20.jpg` and their `_top`/`_bottom` (portrait) or `_left`/`_right` (landscape: p05, p09, p14) halves, plus zoomed crops of small writing and of every drawing.
- **Pages covered:** 1–20 = the whole PDF. 13 handwritten fundus case sheets; most run onto a second page (diagnosis/plan on the next page).
- **Anonymisation:** patient names → `Mr X` / `Mrs X` / `Ms X` (title kept as written); MR numbers → `[number]`; towns → `[place]`; an outside hospital → `[hospital]`; dates → `[date]`. Age and sex kept. "JEH" (the department's own hospital) is kept.

**Heading order used across these sheets (summary)**
1. Name (+ MR No.) → Age/Sex → Chief Complaints. History (DM/HTN/CAD and duration, past IOL surgery, previous Avastin/PRP, night blindness, consanguinity, affected relatives) is jotted **beside** the complaint, not under headings.
2. Vision (RE above LE; unaided → with pinhole; refraction Dvn/Nvn added at the side) → NCT (two values one above the other, eyes not labelled; ± CCT).
3. Anterior Segment as an RE | LE table (Conjunctiva, Cornea, AC, Iris, Pupil, Lens; ± Lids/Eyelids, Sclera).
4. Fundus / POSTERIOR SEGMENT = two labelled colour drawings (RE left, LE right). **No page has a written (prose) fundus description.**
5. [OCT macula / refraction / extra history] → Diagnosis (one eye-prefixed line per condition, retinal diagnosis + lens status) → Plan (written for only 4 of the 13 cases).

Hand A = cursive, headings in black (orange on p17), entries in blue. Hand B = block capitals, headings in red, entries in blue, history/refraction additions in orange or yellow, fundus circles in red. Hand C = cursive, headings black, RE/LE headers, history box and diagnosis in red, fundus circles in blue. The sheets were also filled in by a second writer: history lines in Hand B's print style are added to Hand A and Hand C sheets (pp 8, 11, 12, 20), and cursive history lines are added to Hand B sheets (pp 13, 15). These are marked `[added in another hand: …]`.

**Conventions in this transcript:** `Ⓝ` = circled N (normal); `c̄` = "with" (c with a bar), so "c̄ PH" = with pinhole; `—PH→` = an arrow labelled PH; `< a / b` = two values written on the upper and lower limbs of a bracket (the bracket is not labelled by eye; wherever eyes are labelled on these sheets RE is written above LE); `~~…~~` = struck through on the sheet; `^…^` = inserted above the line; ink colours other than each sheet's usual ones are given in square brackets, e.g. `[red ink]`, `[orange ink]`, `[pencil]`; `[added in another hand: …]` = an addition in handwriting visibly different from the sheet's writer; `[?…]` = uncertain reading; `[illegible]` = cannot be read. **No correction could be attributed to a senior with confidence, so `[senior's note: …]` is not used** (see Format observations). Drawings: the circles are never labelled RE/LE; the eye is inferred from the disc being drawn on the nasal side (right side of the left circle = RE, left side of the right circle = LE), which matches the diagnosis wherever only one eye is affected. Positions are described as on the page (top = superior; away from the disc = temporal). Obvious spellings corrected only (Sluggich → Sluggish, Auastin → Avastin, Consanguinous → Consanguineous, Stargardti → Stargardt's); abbreviations kept as written.

## Format observations

- **One skeleton, three writers.** Every sheet runs identification → Chief Complaints → Vision → NCT → Anterior Segment table → fundus drawing → Diagnosis (→ Plan). Hand A (pp 1–2, 8–9, 12, 17, 20) writes cursive headings in black (orange on p17); Hand B (pp 4–7, 10, 13–16, 18–19) writes block-capital headings in red; Hand C (pp 3, 11) writes black headings with red RE/LE headers and a red diagnosis. The skeleton is the same whoever writes it; only the heading for the fundus differs ("Fundus :" on Hand A's pp 8, 12, 17 but none on pp 1, 20; "POSTERIOR SEGMENT" on every Hand B sheet; none on Hand C's pp 3, 11).
- **History is not written under headings.** Twelve of 13 sheets have no HOPI / past / family history section. DM/HTN/CAD and their duration, treatment (OHA, insulin), past IOL surgery, previous Inj Avastin or PRP sittings, night blindness, consanguinity and affected relatives are jotted to the right of the complaint or vision, often boxed off by a vertical line and often in a second colour (red, orange or yellow). Only Case 9 (p14) has "PAST SURGICAL HISTORY" and "FAMILY HISTORY" headings, placed at the end after the OCT.
- **Vision and IOP lines.** RE above LE; unaided → pinhole written with an arrow, "6/24 —PH→ 6/18" or "6/60 —c̄ PH→ 6/36P" (Hands A and C), or inline, "6/18 c̄ PH 6/12" (Hand B); "P" = partial (6/12P); low vision as CF at 50, HM+, 1/60. Refraction is added at the side as Dvn / Nvn (sph / cyl @ axis → acuity; near add → N-notation; NIPH, NIG), usually in orange or red ink (pp 5, 11, 13, 19, 20). IOP is always "NCT", two values one above the other (on a bracket or on two dashes), with or without mmHg; CCT is written beside it when relevant (pp 3, 20); p15 has empty "AT" and "CCT" headings.
- **Anterior-segment table.** Row labels on the left, eyes as column headers — (RE)/(LE), R/L or RE/LE, sometimes circled in red; p15 has no column headers. Rows are drawn from Lids/Eyelids, Conjunctiva, Cornea, AC, Sclera, Iris, Pupil, Lens, in no fixed order (Pupil first on pp 1, 13, 20, next-to-last on most Hand B sheets, last on p3, and no Pupil row at all on pp 8, 11, 12, 17; Iris before AC on p8; Iris before Cornea on p13). Normal = Ⓝ, "Normal", "N", "N depth", "Clear", "NCP"; pupil = "3mm RTL", "Sluggish RTL", "RTL"; lens = NS-2 / NS-3, IMC (NS-2), ACC, NS-2, Cortical cataract, PCIOL, PxF in ALC.
- **No written fundus description on any of the 20 pages.** There is no distant direct ophthalmoscopy → media → disc → vessels → background → macula → periphery sequence and no "suggestive of" wording. The posterior segment is recorded only as two labelled colour drawings under "Fundus :" (Hand A), "POSTERIOR SEGMENT" (Hand B), or no heading at all (pp 1, 3, 11, 20). The cup–disc ratio appears only as a drawing label ("CDR 0.3", "CD 0.3").
- **Drawing conventions.** RE on the left, LE on the right, never labelled; disc on the nasal side; every lesion is named by a label with a pointer, written between or below the circles, and one label often serves both eyes with two pointers. Label vocabulary used: NVD, NVE, ?NVE, NVE c̄ FVP, preretinal hge / PRH, VH Hazy media, HE, Retinal Hge, Sub ret Hge / Sub retinal Hemorrhage, DRUSEN, Scattered drusen with atrophic changes at macula, Disciform scar, Sclerosed vessel, ST BRVO / STBRVO, AV crossing changes, Lens Haze, bony spicules, PALE DISC, Arteriolar attenuation, Sub retinal Lesion, Retino Choroidal Coloboma, Involving disc & macula, FR seen, PRP marks, PRP 2 sitting given, ERM at macula, ? Stargardt's disease, coloboma. An anterior-segment sketch is added when the lesion is anterior (iris coloboma, p16).
- **Investigations** are written after the drawing as "BE macula OCT — RE … / LE …" (pp 14, 19), or as a red side note "(BE) OCT ([date]) < Disciform scar / Foveal atrophy" (p8).
- **Diagnosis.** Headed "Diagnosis :" or "DIAGNOSIS" — never "provisional diagnosis". One line per condition, each starting with the eye: "(BE)", "(RE)", "(LE)", "BE -", "RE -", "LE -". Whenever the lens is not clear, the retinal diagnosis is paired with a lens line (Senile cataract, Pseudophakia, PCIOL); the two clear-lens cases (pp 1, 17) have the retinal diagnosis alone. Hand A puts the retinal diagnosis first; Hand B usually puts the cataract first. Abbreviations are accepted (PDR, ST BRVO, STBRVO), doubt is shown with "?" (? Micro Cornea), and qualifiers such as "old ST BRVO" are used. No aetiology or stage wording appears beyond the disease name.
- **Plan** is written for only 4 of the 13 cases (pp 2, 5, 7, 10), always eye + treatment: "(BE) PRP", "(LE) Inj Avastin", "LE Sectoral PRP", "LE 3 doses of Avastin injection", "BE PRP". No other investigations or follow-up are planned on these sheets.
- **Corrections.** No signed or clearly senior correction appears, so nothing is marked `[senior's note]`. Struck-through entries are in the writers' own ink (lens "? IMC" → "Clear", p1; "clear" → "NS-2", p4; "Clear" → "Microcornea", p15; an age written after the name and struck out, p6). The sheets were cross-filled: Hand B's print style adds to Hand A and Hand C sheets — p8 (the RE complaint with its duration, "RE Defective vision - 2Y"; "Nil comorbs"), p11 (CAD - 4years; RE Defective vision - 4months), p12 ("since childhood"; "H/o night blindness"; red "K/c/o DM & HTN x 6yrs"; "H/o younger Sibling with RP"; "Non Consanguineous"), p20 (refined diagnosis "choroidal & DISC - RE / LE Retino choroidal Coloboma"). A cursive hand adds to Hand B sheets — p13 (K/C/O HTN; consanguineous parents → both children RP), p15 (since childhood; date of IOL surgery; (RE) ESOtropia). These additions, and the pencil queries (p18 "Disc edema / Pale disc"; p20 "Hirschberg's : RCS / (RE) Abduction limitation"), show what was felt missing: each eye's complaint with its duration, comorbidities with duration (or "Nil comorbs"), night blindness, consanguinity and affected relatives, surgical dates, a squint assessment, and an eye-by-eye diagnosis stating the extent of the coloboma.
- **Colours are given meaning only by the labels — no sheet has a colour legend.** Lesion colours are fairly consistent (below), but the fundus outline depends on the writer: red (Hand B), black ink or pencil (Hand A), blue (Hand C, and p17).

### Proposed MASTER FUNDUS FORMAT (heading order as these sheets use it)

1. **Name** (Mr / Mrs …) with **MR No.**
2. **Age / Sex**
3. **Chief Complaints** — eye + complaint + duration; history written beside it (k/c/o DM / HTN / CAD × duration and treatment; past ocular surgery — eye, when, where; previous Inj Avastin / PRP sittings; night blindness; consanguinity and affected relatives)
4. **Vision** — RE / LE: unaided → c̄ PH ("P" = partial); refraction (Dvn / Nvn) at the side
5. **NCT** — RE / LE (mmHg), with **CCT** (± AT) beside it
6. **Anterior Segment** — RE | LE table: (Lids) · Conjunctiva · Cornea · AC · (Sclera) · Iris · Pupil · Lens
7. *(Ocular motility, when relevant — Hirschberg's, abduction limitation, esotropia: pp 15, 20)*
8. **Fundus** (Hand B: **POSTERIOR SEGMENT**) — labelled colour drawing of both fundi, RE left / LE right, disc nasal, CDR written as a label. These sheets contain no prose fundus description.
9. *(Anterior-segment drawing when the lesion is anterior — iris coloboma, p16)*
10. **Investigations** — OCT macula: RE … / LE … (dated)
11. *(Past surgical history / Family history — only if not already noted, p14)*
12. **Diagnosis** — one eye-prefixed line per condition: retinal diagnosis + lens status
13. **Plan** — eye + treatment (PRP / sectoral PRP / Inj Avastin × doses)

Compared with the usual retinal-drawing code (red = blood and arteries, blue = veins, green = vitreous / media opacity, yellow = exudate, black = pigment), these sheets follow it for lesions. They differ in drawing the fundus outline in red, black, blue or pencil depending on the writer, in drawing vessels in red (p8) or blue (pp 12, 13, 17) without separating arteries from veins, and in adding orange for drusen and yellow for coloboma and sclerosed vessels.
