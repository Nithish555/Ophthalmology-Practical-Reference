- **Source file:** `Case Sheet Proforma.pdf` (uploads › Books › Practicals Reference › Case Format; linked locally as `/home/claude/cards/src/proforma.pdf`). Read from the page photos `/home/claude/cards/img/proforma/p01.jpg`–`p17.jpg` and their `_top` / `_bottom` halves. Pages 8 and 15 are faded photos and were also read from contrast-enhanced crops.
- **Pages covered:** 1–17. Page 1 = decorative cover (no text). Pages 2–6 = bilateral proptosis (thyroid). Pages 7–9 = primary open-angle glaucoma. Pages 10–13 = printed DigiNerve teaching slides (photographed collages). Pages 14–17 = corneal ulcer.
- **Anonymisation:** patient names → `Mr X`. The institution named on the glaucoma sheet → `[place]`. No hospital/IP/OP/MRD numbers, addresses or dates appear on these pages. Age, sex and occupation are kept as written.

**Heading order used across these sheets (summary)**
1. CASE TITLE (capitals, underlined) → identity line (Mr X, age yrs/sex, "… by occupation") → Chief complaint(s) ("came with c/o … for the past …").
2. H/o presenting illness: "Patient was apparently normal … then …", then starred onset / progression / associations, then starred "No H/o …" negatives and "H/o … ⊕" positives.
3. Past history → Personal history → Family history → Treatment history (the corneal-ulcer sheet puts Family before Personal) → General examination (+ Vitals) → Systemic examination (CVS, RS, P/A, CNS).
4. Case-specific block (Examination of Neck / Local examination of eye) → Ocular examination table (RE | LE) → Fundus (RE | LE) → special work-up (Proptosis evaluation, Hertel's, differential IOP; Gonioscopy by modified Shaffer; "Diagram with parts").
5. Last line: "My provisional diagnosis:" / "DIAGNOSIS:" — laterality first, then the lesion, then "probably of … etiology / related …" and the stage or control status.

**Conventions in this transcript:** `c̄` = "with" (written as c with a bar); `⊕` = circled plus (present / positive); `Ⓝ` = circled N (normal); `(+/-)`, `___` and "a/b" alternatives are the sheet's own fill-in slots; lines starred on the sheet are given as Markdown list items (`* …`), `★` where a larger drawn star is used; `~~…~~` = struck through on the sheet; `[senior's note: …]` = senior's correction in **orange ink** unless another colour is stated; `[?…]` = uncertain reading; `[illegible]` = cannot be read. Obvious spellings corrected only (e.g. apetite → appetite); abbreviations kept as written.

## Format observations

- **History order:** CASE TITLE → identity line → Chief complaint(s) → H/o presenting illness → Past history → Personal → Family → Treatment history → General examination with vitals → Systemic examination. The corneal-ulcer sheet puts Family before Personal. The proptosis sheet adds "Examination of Neck" before the eye. The corneal-ulcer sheet adds "Local examination of eye" (lagophthalmos, ectropion/entropion, trichiasis, blepharitis, meibomitis, foreign body in fornices, lid closure, Bell's) before the table.
- **Identity and chief complaint wording:** "Mr X, 70yrs old Male has come for … for Regular follow-up" or "Mr X, 35yrs old male, labourer by occupation came with chief complaints of Pain, Redness, watering, defective vision (LE) for the past 10days". Laterality goes in brackets after the symptoms.
- **HOPI template:** "Patient was apparently normal [time] back/before, then he noticed/had … which was" + starred descriptors in a fixed order (onset → progression → associated symptoms). Then a starred block of case-specific negatives ("No H/o …"), with positives marked "⊕" (e.g. "H/o palpitation ⊕").
- **Past, personal and family history are checklists:** "K/c/o DM/SHTN/TB/Asthma/Epilepsy for ___ on treatment ___", "H/o similar complaints in the past", "H/o cataract / any ocular surgery". Personal history is always the triad: smoker/alcoholic, mixed diet, normal bowel and bladder habits. Family history is "No H/o similar illness in family members" or "Not significant". Treatment history names each drug with strength and frequency (T. Carbimazole 5mg …, Bimatoprost e/D … HS).
- **General and systemic examination wording:** "Pt is conscious, oriented, afebrile / moderately built and nourished; Not anemic, Not icteric, No cyanosis, No clubbing, No generalized lymphadenopathy". Vitals: "PR → 76/min, regular in rhythm, normal in volume; RR; BP … measured in left arm in sitting posture". Systemic: "CVS → S1S2 heard, No murmur; RS → NVBS, no added sounds; P/A → soft, no organomegaly; CNS → No focal neurological deficit (NFND)".
- **The RE | LE table is a hand-ruled 3-column grid:** capitalised row labels on the left, then RE, then LE. Row order: VISUAL ACUITY → EOM → Tn by GAT → LIDS → CONJUNCTIVA → CORNEA → AC (ANTR. CHAMBER) → IRIS → PUPIL → LENS. Fundus is written below the table with RE and LE side by side, or as "BE)" when both eyes are the same.
- **Cell shorthand:** "6/9 c̄ +0.50 Dsph 6/6", "6/60 NIGNIP", "10 mmHg" (GAT), "Ⓝ" / "Normal" / "clear", "VH - Gr IV (ND)", "CPN", "RTL (3mm)", "Pharmacologically dilated", "PCIOL". Fundus: "Media clear, Disc & vessels appear normal, Macula FR ⊕". A lesion is written as a starred list inside the affected eye's cell (colour, size in mm, margins, location, surrounding infiltrates; hypopyon "(+/-) ___ mm, Mobile/Not mobile").
- **Case-specific work-up blocks:** Proptosis is described On Inspection (signs with the eponym in capitals in brackets: DALRYMPLE'S, ENROTH, MOBIUS, STELLWAG'S; Naffziger's) → On palpation (finger insinuation in all 4 quadrants, compressible, reducible, retropulsion, thrill/pulsation) → On auscultation (bruit). This is followed by Hertel's (base 110; RE/LE mm) and a differential IOP table (primary, upward and downward gaze). Glaucoma has "Gonioscopy: By Modified Shaffer's grading" drawn as an X-cross per eye with the grade in each quadrant. The glaucoma fundus follows this order: media → disc shape → margins → CDR → nasalization → bayonetting → PPA → NRR thinning (sup/inf) → notching → tessellation.
- **Diagnosis phrasing:** "My provisional diagnosis:" (or "DIAGNOSIS:"), laterality first (B/L, (BE), (LE)) → lesion → "probably of … etiology" or "… related" → stage or status. Examples: "B/L Axial Proptosis probably of Thyroid related Orbitopathy in Active stage"; "(BE) primary open angle Glaucoma with Pseudophakia with Intraocular Pressure under control with Medical Management"; "(LE) Paracentral corneal ulcer, Probably of Bacterial Etiology in Healing stage".
- **Seniors' corrections (orange) show what examiners want:**
  - Glaucoma table: EOM inserted after visual acuity, the CCT row struck out, "VH" circled.
  - Glaucoma: "field (Confrontation test)" added before the diagnosis, and "c̄ vomiting and pain" added to the headache negative.
  - Glaucoma: key HOPI descriptors and the gonioscopy method underlined, and a drug/systemic history checklist added (sulfa and other drug allergy, med h/o, head injury, asthma, CVD, migraine, Raynaud, PVD, steroid).
  - Corneal ulcer: a full ulcer description checklist added (site, size, shape, depth, colour, margin, floor, vascularisation, KPs, stromal infiltrate) and bedside tests (sensation, staining, syringing, digital Tn).
  - Corneal ulcer viva notes: Kimura spatula, the 4 stages of ulcer, antifungal choice by depth and fungus type, fortified antibiotic strengths, and the actions of the cycloplegic.
- **These "proforma" sheets are model answers with blanks:** fill-in slots and alternatives are left in ("___ yrs", "(+/-)", "adequate/inadequate", "Paracentral/central", "Mobile/Not mobile", "(Active/Inactive depends on signs)"). They are meant to be adapted to the case on the day.
- **The DigiNerve slides set the department's description scripts as "Feature – finding" lines:**
  - AC depth by pen-torch: location of torch, area of iris illumination, grade.
  - AC depth by Van Herick: slit 1 mm inside the temporal limbus at 60º, ratio, grade.
  - Bleb: height, clarity, horizontal extent, location, vascularity, microcysts, subconjunctival fibrosis, sutures.
  - PI: margin, site in clock hours, surrounding iris, retro-illumination patency.
  - NVI: appearance, character, course, arrangement, extent in clock hours.
  - Pseudoexfoliation: colour, nature, location, iris atrophy.
  - Glaucomatous disc: size, disc shape, cup shape, margins, colour, CDR, NRR, vessels, AV ratio, peripapillary changes, RNFL defects.
- **Notation:** every finding is a starred line; "⊕" marks present; "c̄" means with; "→" links a system to its finding; headings are underlined and end with ":". All drawings are plain black pen with no colour; only the seniors' notes are in colour (orange, plus one green mark on p4).
