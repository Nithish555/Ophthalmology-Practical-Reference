**Pages covered:** 18–31 (1-based PDF pages). What is actually on them:
- 18–20: uveitis long case (acute iridocyclitis).
- 21: a **separate fundus write-up, "1) Optic atrophy" (RE)**. It is not part of the uveitis case.

**Anonymisation:** 3 patient names were replaced with Mr X / Mrs X. No hospital, IP, OP or MRD numbers, places or dates appear on these pages. Printed letterhead fragments, cut off at the top edge of pp. 20 and 30, are not transcribed.

**Heading order used across these sheets (summary)**
1. Title (underlined), then the opening line "<Name> <age> yrs <sex> came with c/o <complaint> for the past <duration>", then starred qualifiers (onset, pain, worse on gaze).
2. Positive "H/o … ⊕" lines, then a case-specific "NO H/o …" screen (with teaching notes in brackets).
3. Past H/o → Family H/o → Personal H/o → Treatment H/o. The 6th-nerve sheet goes Past → Treatment → Personal and has no Family H/o.
4. General Examination → Vitals → Systemic Examination (CVS / RS / P/A / CNS) → Local Examination (ptosis and 6th nerve only).
5. Ocular Examination RE | LE table → Fundus ((RE)/(LE) or (BE)) → case-specific table (Evaluation of ptosis; Examination of other Cranial Nerves) → "My Provisional Diagnosis:" / "My Diagnosis is:".
6. Fundus write-ups: distant direct ophthalmoscopy (red glow) → direct ophthalmoscopy (media) → disc → vessels → each lesion "… s/o <lesion>" → macula → background retina / IDO / +90D slit-lamp confirmation.

**Conventions in this transcript:**
- Each page's running text is in a `text` block, to keep the original line breaks, indents and `*` bullets.
- `_word_` = underlined in the original.
- `⊕` = circled plus (present / positive). `Ⓝ` = circled N (normal).
- `c̄` = "with" (c-bar).
- `·` separates separate lines written inside one table cell.
- `[edge: …]` = part of a neighbouring page caught at the photo margin. It is not transcribed further.
- `[senior's note: …]` = coloured-ink addition.
- All writing is in black/dark ballpoint unless stated otherwise.

## Format observations

- **Opening line and complaint.** Each case opens with "<Name> <age> yrs (old) Male came with c/o …" (or "<Name> 53/F came with complaints of …"). The complaint is qualified with starred lines (onset, pain, worse on gaze), and the key descriptor is underlined (_Sudden_, _Acute_, _pain_, _Drooping_, _6months_, _left_).
- **History lists.** Positive findings come first as "H/o … ⊕". A long, case-specific "NO H/o …" screen follows:
  - Uveitis: joint pain/backache, diarrhoea, rash/oral/genital ulcer, poliosis, groin lymph nodes, cough.
  - Ptosis: diurnal variation, dysphagia, dysphonia, numbered ①②③.
  - 6th nerve: headache/vomiting, fever, trauma, hemiparesis, other cranial nerves (bracketed), scalp tenderness/jaw claudication.
  - Short bracketed teaching tags say why each item is asked: "(Associated syndromes)", "(Giant cell arteritis)", "(To Diff Monoocular & Binocular diplopia)".
- **Templates.** Past / Family / Personal / Treatment history are partly fill-in templates ("for the past __ yrs", "on Treatment ___", "H/o ocular surgery before __ yr at ___", "If present, Ask Duration and Treatment H/o"). Headings are underlined and end in a colon.
- **Stock general and systemic examination.**
  - General: "Pt is conscious, oriented to time, place"; "Moderately built and nourished"; "No pallor / icterus / clubbing / cyanosis / lymphadenopathy / pedal edema".
  - Vitals: PR with "Regular in rhythm, Normal in volume"; BP "measured in left arm in sitting posture"; RR.
  - Systemic one-liners: "CVS – S1 S2 heard, No murmurs; RS – NVBS, No added sounds; P/A – Soft, No organomegaly; CNS – NFND / No focal neurological deficit".
- **RE | LE table layout.** Each sheet uses a hand-ruled 3-column table: row labels in the left column, with "(RE)" and "(LE)" written above the eye columns (circled on the 6th-nerve sheet). The "OCULAR Examination:" heading sits just above the table. Row order changes with the case:
  - Uveitis: VA → Tn by GAT → Lids → Conjunctiva → Cornea → AC → Iris → Pupil → Lens.
  - Ptosis: VA → Eyebrows → EOM → Lid → Conjunctiva → Cornea (+ sensation) → AC → Iris → Pupil → Lens → Tn by GAT → Fields.
  - 6th nerve: V.A → Lids → Tn by GAT → Ocular Movements → Conjunctiva … Lens.
- **Cell shorthand.**
  - Ⓝ = normal and ⊕ = present; CPN (iris), RTL / "3mm (RTL)" (pupil), ND (AC depth), "clear", FR ⊕.
  - Visual acuity is written as unaided acuity, then the correction (sphere over cylinder), then the corrected acuity: "6/36 c̄ +2.00 DSph/+1.75 D cyl × 160° 6/9".
  - IOP is written as "12 mmHg", with the method in the row label ("Tn by GAT"). AC is graded "cells 2⊕ / flare 2⊕".
  - Abnormal findings are written out in the cell ("Circumcorneal congestion", "Abduction restriction ⊕", "Ptosis").
- **Special evaluation tables use a mirror layout:** RE value | parameter | LE value.
  - Ptosis: MRD I with the scale "(+2/+1/0/-1/-2)", MCD, VPH, levator excursion/function "Adequate (good)/poor/fair", skin crease, frontalis overaction, lid position, lid closure, Bell's phenomenon, lagophthalmos, jaw winking. All measurements are in mm.
  - Cranial nerves: right-side finding | Roman numeral I–XII | left-side finding, each nerve with one or two stock test phrases.
- **Ptosis measurements are not in the RE | LE table.** That table only says "Lid: Normal | Ptosis". The numbers are in the separate "Evaluation of ptosis" table (RE 5/9/9/14 mm vs LE 2/9/6/10 mm for MRD I / MCD / VPH / levator).
- **Fundus description formula.**
  - Sequence: "On distant direct ophthalmoscopy (at 1 arm distance) shows a (good) red glow" → "Direct ophthalmoscopy close to face reveals clear media" → disc (vertically oval, normal in size, well-defined margins, pink, CDR 0.3, healthy NRR) → "Vessels arising from the centre of the disc branching dichotomously, AV ratio 2:3" → each lesion as morphology + location + "s/o <named lesion>" → Macula (FR ⊕ / absent, oedema) → "Background retina appears normal" → "IDO shows peripheries to be normal" / "+90D slit-lamp biomicroscopy confirmed".
  - In the long cases the fundus is shortened to "Media clear / Disc & vessels app normal / Macula FR ⊕".
- **No fundus drawings and no colour anywhere in pp. 18–31.** The diabetic retinopathy page (30) and the optic atrophy page (21) are text only, in black ink. Colour appears only as words ("pale yellow and waxy", "yellowish", "linear red", "fluffy yellow", "pink in colour"), and key lesion words are underlined.
- **No diplopia charting on the sixth-nerve sheet.** Motility is recorded only in words: history ("It worsens on left lateral gaze"; the diplopia subsides when either eye is occluded), local examination ("characteristic face turn to left side"), the table ("Ocular Movements: full | Abduction restriction ⊕") and the CN table ("EOM: full | III, IV, VI | …"). There is no gaze diagram, no prism/cover values, and no diagnosis line.
