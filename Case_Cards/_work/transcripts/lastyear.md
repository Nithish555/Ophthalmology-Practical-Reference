**How to read the tables**

- Text in **[square brackets]** is my expansion or note. Text in (round brackets) is written on the sheet.
- "—" means nothing is written.
- Diagnoses keep the spelling and capitals used on the sheet. Trailing full stops are dropped. Eye markers (RE/LE/BE) and "case sheet" notes are moved into their own columns.
- ✓ and ✗ are tick and cross marks drawn above a task word. Their meaning is not explained on the sheet.

The fundus sheet (photo 6, #13) lists the same patient under fundus as plain "Coloboma". The expected summary follows the category sheets (fundus 16, AS 5). By the master list it would be **fundus 15, anterior segment 6**.
2. **"Long case" is never written.** The sheets say **"case sheet"**: master #24 (PACG) and master #33 (fungal corneal ulcer, RE). Reading these as long cases is an inference. Neither the glaucoma sheet nor the AS sheet marks them. The expected summary's "**the** long case" also hides that two cases were marked.
3. **Gonioscopy 8 counts every time "Gonio" is written.** One entry (PACG, row 8) says "**needs to be done**", so it was apparently not done. Another (PACG, row 6, the case-sheet case) has a **cross ✗** over Gonio. Only **5** gonioscopy entries are ticked. (AT is ticked 2 of 8; HFA 2 of 3.)
4. **There is only one gonioscopy diagram.** It sits beside **case 1 (POAG)**, whose only listed task is AT, and the sheet does not say which case it belongs to. If it is case 1's, gonioscopy would be **9**, not 8. "Deepest structure seen per quadrant" is the usual convention, but it is not written on the sheet. The diagram simply shows SS in all four quadrants (an open angle), for BE.
5. **The CRVO glaucoma case** is written only as "CRVO" on the glaucoma sheet. "CRVO c̄ POAG" appears on the master list.
6. **The hemi-CRVO + POAG case** is circled **both F and G** on the master list and noted "AGM". It appears only on the fundus sheet. The expected summary lists it under fundus only, which matches the sheets but loses the dual tag.
7. **One "fungal corneal ulcer"** is written only as **"Fungal ulcer"** on both sheets; the cornea is not stated.
8. **Laterality is missing from the expected summary** except for Stargardt. The photos also record: dislocated lens in vitreous **RE**, failed graft **RE**, fungal corneal ulcer (case-sheet case) **RE**, one STBRVO **LE**, one POAG **BE**, and the gonio diagram **BE**.

## 5. Other points on the photos that help with preparation

- **Glaucoma cases come with a task.** AT was written for 8 of 12 cases and gonioscopy for 8 of 12, including **every PACG**. HFA was written for 3: the CRVO case, a POAG case on AGM, and the POAG with pseudoexfoliation.
- **Be ready to draw a gonioscopy diagram.** Use one cross per eye, splitting the angle into superior, inferior, nasal and temporal quadrants, and write the structure seen in each quadrant (e.g. SS = scleral spur). Writing the deepest visible structure is the usual convention; the sheet does not state it.
- **Treatment status was recorded.** Notes say "AGM" (on anti-glaucoma medication) or "no AGM". Ask about and state the current anti-glaucoma drugs when you present a glaucoma case.
- **"Case sheet" cases** were a PACG and a fungal corneal ulcer (RE). Practise writing a full case sheet for a glaucoma case and for a corneal ulcer.
- **Fundus was the biggest group (16 of 34).**
  - Most common: RP (4) and PDR (3).
  - Two each: ARMD, STBRVO and coloboma.
  - One each: hemi-CRVO with POAG, Stargardt's disease (BE) and dislocated lens in vitreous (RE).
- **Anterior segment cases:** fungal corneal ulcer ×2, failed graft (RE), subluxated lens (Marfan's), and lamellar cataract / aphakia.
- **Cranial nerve:** one 3rd CN palsy. No task is written for it.
- **Marks with no stated meaning:** ✓/✗ above task words; small dash marks beside glaucoma case numbers 4, 6, 7, 10 and 11; a red dot beside case 3 (NVG); the heavily circled number 7; and white smudges over row 12. No ages are written on any list.
- **Older scheme (photo 1):**
  - Fundus and refraction are separate stations (1 × (10 + 5) min each).
  - There are also 5 ward-round cases (3 + 2 min each) and 5 OSCE stations (2 min each).
  - Viva includes competency assessment (60 + 20 = 80). The log book carries 20 marks.
  - Pass mark is 150/300 (50%).
