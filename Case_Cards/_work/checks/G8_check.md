### Verified unchanged (the checks the brief asked for)
- **Normal range and why 21**:
  - Normal 10–21 mm Hg (ARAVIND fitz 204 Q1).
  - Mean 15.5 ± 2.57 mm Hg; mean + 2 standard deviations ≈ 20.5; about 95% of a Gaussian curve; distribution skewed to the right; the limits are only a rough approximation (ARAVIND fitz 204 Q2).
- **3.06 mm and the dial**:
  - At 3.06 mm, corneal rigidity balances the capillary attraction of the tear meniscus (ARAVIND fitz 209 Q36; BAIDYA fitz 753).
  - At 3.06 mm, 0.1 g = 1 mm Hg (ARAVIND fitz 209 Q35).
  - Dial reading × 10 = IOP; worked example 1.8 → 18 mm Hg (BAIDYA fitz 753 h).
  - Dial set at 1 (10 mm Hg), never at 0, because microvibrations can cause corneal erosions (ARAVIND fitz 209 Q39; BAIDYA fitz 753 e).
- **Fluorescein and the slit lamp**: fluorescein 0.25% (ARAVIND fitz 209 Q37) or a strip (BAIDYA fitz 753 a); cobalt blue filter, maximal illumination, light and microscope 60° apart (BAIDYA fitz 753 c; ARAVIND fitz 209 Q38).
- **Disinfection agents**: 70% isopropyl alcohol, 3% hydrogen peroxide, diluted sodium hypochlorite (1:10) (ARAVIND fitz 210 Q45; BAIDYA fitz 753).
- **CCT**:
  - Mean 520 µm; thick corneas over-read, thin corneas under-read, about 0.7 mm Hg per 10 µm; oedema reads falsely low (ARAVIND fitz 210 Q43 i; BAIDYA fitz 754 b, c).
  - The viva arithmetic is correct: (600 − 520)/10 × 0.7 = 5.6 mm Hg, and 26 − 5.6 ≈ 20 mm Hg.
- **Astigmatism**:
  - Over 3 dioptres: use the red-line method, or average two readings with the prism turned 90° for the second (BAIDYA fitz 754 d).
  - With-the-rule under-reads, against-the-rule over-reads, about 1 mm Hg per 4 dioptres (ARAVIND fitz 210 Q43 iii).
- **Mires**: green (BAIDYA fitz 753 f), as CONSISTENCY §5 decides. ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q40) says yellow.
- **Endpoint**: the semicircles are equal and their inner edges coincide (ARAVIND fitz 209 Q41; BAIDYA fitz 753 g).
- **Error sources**:
  - Mires too thick or too thin (BAIDYA fitz 754 a).
  - A wider meniscus or improper vertical alignment reads falsely high (ARAVIND fitz 210 Q43 ii).
  - Lid squeezing raises IOP through orbicularis tone (ARAVIND fitz 206 Q13).
  - The Valsalva manoeuvre raises IOP through episcleral venous pressure (Q12).
  - Irregular cornea, and readings after LASIK (ARAVIND Q43 v–vi; BAIDYA fitz 754 g).
  - Repeated readings fall slightly through a massaging effect (BAIDYA fitz 754 f).
  - Calibration is checked with the manufacturer's calibration arm every session (BAIDYA fitz 754 e).
- **Indications and other tonometers**:
  - GAT is reliable, reproducible and not influenced by scleral rigidity (ARAVIND fitz 209 Q42).
  - Tonopen (ARAVIND fitz 211 Q48; BAIDYA fitz 754).
  - Tonometers for corneal scarring (ARAVIND fitz 210 Q47).
  - Perkins for bed-bound or anaesthetised patients (BAIDYA fitz 753).
  - Non-contact tonometer for screening, no anaesthetic needed (BAIDYA fitz 754).
- **Diurnal variation**: 3–6 mm Hg in normal eyes vs 10 mm Hg or more in glaucoma; the IOP peak comes 3–4 hours after the cortisol peak (ARAVIND fitz 205 Q8–9).
- **Viva**:
  - Imbert–Fick law (ARAVIND fitz 208 Q31; BAIDYA fitz 753).
  - Schiotz rigidity errors (BAIDYA fitz 752).
  - Schiotz method (BAIDYA fitz 752–753) and weights 5.5, 7.5, 10 and 15 g (ARAVIND fitz 207 Q25).
  - Infections a tonometer can spread (ARAVIND fitz 210 Q46).
  - Dynamic contour tonometer (PASCAL): reading independent of corneal thickness, no fluorescein, digital display, contour-matching surface (ARAVIND fitz 211 Q53; BAIDYA fitz 754).
