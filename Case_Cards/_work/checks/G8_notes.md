# G8 notes — Applanation tonometry: perform and record

Packet: ARAVIND fitz 204–211 (= printed p.178–185, section 4.1) · BAIDYA fitz 751–754 (= printed p.737–740) ·
TYPED FORMAT — GLAUCOMA fitz 0–1. Card word count (wc -w): 744 (budget 550–750).

## Claims ledger

### Indications
- IOP is part of every glaucoma examination — TYPED FORMAT fitz 1 ("Intraocular pressure" in the ocular examination list); house case sheet "Tn by GAT" row (CARD_SPEC §5)
- GAT: reliable, accurate, reproducible, not influenced by scleral rigidity — ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q42)
- Tonopen for corneal epithelial irregularity, bandage contact lens, oedematous and scarred cornea, nystagmus and head tremor — ARAVIND fitz 211 (Q48); BAIDYA fitz 754
- Corneal scarring: non-contact, Mackay–Marg, Tonopen, pneumatic tonometers — ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q47)
- Perkins: portable, hand-held applanation tonometer for bed-bound or anaesthetised patients — BAIDYA fitz 753
- Non-contact tonometer: no contact, no topical anaesthesia, useful for community screening — BAIDYA fitz 754

### Instrument check
- Calibration checked before each clinical session with the manufacturer's calibration arm; wrong calibration gives false readings — BAIDYA fitz 754 (e)
- Dial set at 1 (= 10 mm Hg) — ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q39); BAIDYA fitz 753 (e)
- Dial at 0 → microvibrations → corneal erosions — ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q39)
- Astigmatism over 3 dioptres: red line on the tonometer housing aligned with the minus axis of the prescription, or average of two readings with the prism rotated 90° for the second — BAIDYA fitz 754 (d); ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q44, different wording, see Disagreements)

### Steps
- Observed-station checklist (consent, patient instruction, tonometer checked, topical drops applied, handling, Schiotz conversion chart referred to) — candidate's university regulation pages, as given in the brief (not a packet source)
- Introduce yourself, explain, consent — required by CARD_SPEC Template D and the regulation checklist (not a packet fact)
- "Forehead against the headband" — BAIDYA fitz 753 (b: "head is firmly attached to the headband")
- "Do not squeeze" — rationale ARAVIND fitz 206 (Q13: hard lid squeezing raises IOP, orbicularis tone)
- "Breathe normally" — rationale ARAVIND fitz 206 (Q12: Valsalva raises IOP via episcleral venous pressure)
- "Look straight ahead", "keep both eyes open", "chin on the rest" — wording from the brief; no packet text (see Omitted)
- Disinfectants: 70% isopropyl alcohol, 3% hydrogen peroxide, diluted sodium hypochlorite (1:10) — ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q45); BAIDYA fitz 753
- Topical anaesthetic drops and fluorescein (strip) into the conjunctival sac — BAIDYA fitz 753 (a)
- Fluorescein 0.25% — ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q37)
- Cobalt blue filter — ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q37); BAIDYA fitz 753 (c)
- Maximal illumination intensity — BAIDYA fitz 753 (c)
- Illumination and microscope about 60° apart — ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q38, "approximately 60°"); BAIDYA fitz 753 (c)
- Separate lids gently, no pressure on the eyeball — BAIDYA fitz 753 (Schiotz step c); ARAVIND fitz 208 (Q28, Schiotz: avoid pressure over the eyelids). Applied to GAT by me.
- Prism advanced until it just touches the corneal apex; two green semicircular mires, one above and one below the horizontal midline — BAIDYA fitz 753 (d, f)
- Endpoint: semicircles equal in size, inner sides coincide — ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q41); BAIDYA fitz 753 (g)
- Dial reading × 10 = IOP in mm Hg — BAIDYA fitz 753 (h)
- Avoid quick repeated readings (massaging effect, slight fall) — BAIDYA fitz 754 (f)
- Disinfect after use — implied by ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q45 agents, Q46 infections); the packet does not state the timing

### How to record
- Format: eye, method, value, time — from the brief; house rows "Tn by GAT (mm Hg)" and "(CCT µm)" from CARD_SPEC §5
- Example values (not clinical claims): RE 18 / LE 16 mm Hg at 10:30 am (the brief's example); dial 1.8 → 18 mm Hg (× 10 rule, BAIDYA fitz 753 h); 600 − 520 = 80 µm in the viva CCT example
- Commonest diurnal pattern: highest in the morning, lowest in the evening (rises again at night) — ARAVIND fitz 205 (Q8)
- Diurnal swing 3–6 mm Hg in normal eyes; 10 mm Hg or more in glaucomatous eyes — ARAVIND fitz 205 (Q8)
- Peak IOP 3–4 hours after the plasma cortisol peak (adrenocortical steroid cycle) — ARAVIND fitz 205 (Q9)

### Normal values and interpretation
- Normal IOP 10–21 mm Hg — ARAVIND fitz 204 (Q1)
- Mean 15.5 ± 2.57 mm Hg; 2 standard deviations above the mean ≈ 20.5 mm Hg; about 95% of a Gaussian curve within mean ± 2 standard deviations; distribution skewed to the right; limits only a rough approximation — ARAVIND fitz 204 (Q2)
- Imbert–Fick law, P = F/A, ideal dry thin-walled sphere — ARAVIND fitz 208 (Q31); BAIDYA fitz 753
- 3.06 mm: corneal rigidity balanced by the capillary attraction of the tear meniscus — ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q36); BAIDYA fitz 753
- At 3.06 mm, 0.1 g corresponds to 1 mm Hg — ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q35)
- CCT mean 520 µm; thicker cornea overestimates, thinner underestimates; average error 0.7 mm Hg per 10 µm — ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q43 i); BAIDYA fitz 754 (b)
- Corneal oedema reads falsely low — ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q43 i); BAIDYA fitz 754 (c)

### Common errors
- Inappropriate fluorescein pattern: mires too thick → falsely high, too thin → falsely low — BAIDYA fitz 754 (a); ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q43 ii: wider meniscus → falsely higher)
- Improper vertical alignment → falsely higher — ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q43 ii); "unequal semicircles" ties to the endpoint "equal in size", ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q41)
- Lid squeezing raises IOP (orbicularis tone) — ARAVIND fitz 206 (Q13)
- Valsalva manoeuvre raises IOP (episcleral venous pressure) — ARAVIND fitz 206 (Q12)
- Astigmatism: with-the-rule underestimated, against-the-rule overestimated, about 1 mm Hg per 4 dioptres — ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q43 iii); BAIDYA fitz 754 (d: distorted mires)
- Corneal irregularity: GAT not useful — ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q43 v)
- After corneal refractive surgery: GAT not accurate — ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q43 vi); BAIDYA fitz 754 (g)
- Repeated readings over a short period → slight fall (massaging effect) — BAIDYA fitz 754 (f)

### Viva
- Imbert–Fick statement — ARAVIND fitz 208 (Q31); BAIDYA fitz 753
- 3.06 mm reason, and 0.1 g = 1 mm Hg — ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q35, Q36); BAIDYA fitz 753
- Fluorescein lets you see the tear meniscus at the margin of the cornea–biprism contact — ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q40)
- CCT 600 µm example: (600 − 520)/10 × 0.7 = 5.6 mm Hg; 26 − 5.6 ≈ 20 mm Hg — arithmetic on ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q43 i)
- Schiotz falsely low with low scleral rigidity (high myopia, after ocular surgery), falsely high with high rigidity (high hypermetropia) — BAIDYA fitz 752; ARAVIND fitz 208 (Q27 i, high myopia only)
- Infections: adenovirus (epidemic keratoconjunctivitis), herpes simplex virus type 1, human immunodeficiency virus, hepatitis B — ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q46)
- Schiotz: supine patient — BAIDYA fitz 752; zero on the metal plate, lids separated without pressure on the eyeball, footplate on the anaesthetised cornea, scale reading converted with the conversion chart — BAIDYA fitz 753; weights 5.5 g fixed, 7.5, 10, 15 g — ARAVIND fitz 207 (Q25)
- Dynamic contour tonometer (PASCAL): reading independent of corneal thickness, no fluorescein, digital display — ARAVIND fitz 211 (Q53); contour-matching surface, digital display — BAIDYA fitz 754

## Disagreements
- **Colour of the mires**: BAIDYA fitz 753 says "two green semi-circular mires"; ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q40) says "2 yellow semi-circles". The card uses **green** (Baidya, the newer book). The viva answer avoids naming a colour.
- **Fluorescein form**: BAIDYA fitz 753 says strip; ARAVIND fitz 209 says 0.25% dye. These are two forms, not a real conflict, so the card gives both.
- **Astigmatism rule**: BAIDYA fitz 754 gives a threshold (over 3 dioptres) and says to align the red line on the housing with the minus axis, or to average two readings at 90°. ARAVIND fitz 210 (Q44) gives no threshold and says to rotate so the axis of least corneal curvature is "opposite the red line on the prism holder". The intent is the same. The card uses Baidya's wording and threshold.
- **520 µm**: ARAVIND fitz 210 calls it the mean; BAIDYA fitz 754 uses it as the cut-off for "thinner". These agree. The card says "mean 520 µm".
- **Schiotz rigidity errors**: ARAVIND fitz 208 gives only high myopia (underestimates). BAIDYA fitz 752 adds after ocular surgery (low) and high hypermetropia (high). There is no conflict; the card uses Baidya.

## Omitted / not in the packet
- **Prism at 0° for routine use**: not in the packet. Only the astigmatism red-line rule is there.
- **Slit-lamp magnification and slit width**: not in the packet. Only cobalt blue, maximal illumination and 60° are.
- **Name and strength of the topical anaesthetic**: not in the packet. The card says "topical anaesthetic drop".
- **Disinfection method details** (soak or contact time, rinsing, drying, timing before or after each patient): not in the packet, which lists agents only. "Disinfect again after use" is implied, not stated.
- **Routine repeat-and-average for GAT**: not in the packet. Averaging appears only for astigmatism over 3 dioptres (two readings, 90° apart) and for the non-contact tonometer (at least three readings). The card says to avoid quick repeats (massaging effect).
- **Patient-instruction wording** ("look straight ahead", "keep both eyes open", "chin on the rest"): from the brief, not the packet. It was kept because the station ticks "patient instruction". "Breath-holding or straining" is my plain-English version of the packet's "Valsalva manoeuvre".
- **Pressure on the lids or globe raising the reading**: the packet has this as a Schiotz precaution, and says lid squeezing raises IOP. The direction for finger pressure is inferred from the squeezing mechanism.
- **"Too much / too little fluorescein"**: the packet's wording is "inappropriate fluorescein pattern: too thick or too thin mires". The card uses the packet wording and does not state the dose–mire link.
- **Volume of aqueous displaced**: ARAVIND fitz 209 (Q33) says "approximately 0.5 mm volume", with a garbled unit, so it is left off.
- **Contraindications** (e.g. active infection, corneal ulcer) and **after-care** (e.g. not rubbing the anaesthetised eye): not in the packet.
- **In the packet but dropped to stay within 750 words**:
  - Tonopen reads lower than GAT below 24 mm Hg and higher above (BAIDYA fitz 754)
  - Tonopen in the operating theatre and in any position (ARAVIND fitz 211; BAIDYA fitz 754)
  - Variable-force vs variable-area applanation tonometers (ARAVIND fitz 208 Q30)
  - Four diurnal curve types (ARAVIND fitz 205 Q10)
  - Posture: supine raises IOP by 0.3–6 mm Hg (ARAVIND fitz 205 Q11)
  - Non-contact tonometer principle and drawbacks (ARAVIND fitz 211 Q49–50)
  - Newer tonometers: rebound, Corvis ST, optical response analyser, phosphene (ARAVIND fitz 211; BAIDYA fitz 754)
  - Schiotz advantages and the Moses effect (ARAVIND fitz 208)
  - Digital tonometry and tonography, C value 0.22–0.30 µL/min/mm Hg (ARAVIND fitz 207)
