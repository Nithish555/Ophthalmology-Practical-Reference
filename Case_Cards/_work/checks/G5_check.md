# G5 fact-check — Neovascular glaucoma, including rubeosis iridis

## 1. Summary
- **Claims checked:** about 300. That covers every number, dose, strength, frequency, stage, grade, named sign, classification and diagnosis line, plus every factual statement in the prose, tables, viva, traps, short case, say-it and quick recall.
- **Verified unchanged:** about 285. **Corrected:** 12 claims. **Removed:** 2 superseded or misleading items. Also 1 format fix (gonioscopy diagram) and 1 `@readmore` update.
- **Final word count (`wc -w`):** 3,120 (budget 2,300–3,200). Viva: 22 Q/A pairs. Say-it: 228 words, with no abbreviations or digits.
- **Format:** headings and sub-headings in template order; every `:::` box closed; every table row has the right number of cells; all `@widths` sum to 100; at most 4 columns; every `Q:` has an `A:`. British spelling throughout. No book names or page numbers in the body.
- **Cidamex (CONSISTENCY §9):** already correct. The printed word is kept, glossed once as "(oral acetazolamide)" — MNEMONICS fitz 53.
- **Gonioscopy cross (CONSISTENCY §2):** rewritten to show the deepest structure only (`SS`, `Closed`). Caption now states the conclusion, in the same style as G1, G4 and G9.
- **Inferences in the writer's notes, now found in the full books:**
  - "Sudden earlier loss of vision → ischaemic CRVO / CRAO": BAIDYA fitz 278 (CRVO: loss is usually sudden, unilateral, painless); fitz 290 (CRAO: sudden, monocular, painless).
  - "No precipitating factor → against primary acute angle closure": BAIDYA fitz 162 (precipitating factors listed under PACG); fitz 190 (an NVG attack may have none).
- **Book disagreements the writer resolved correctly (verified, no change):**
  - Prednisolone acetate 1% 6–8 times a day (BAIDYA fitz 193), over four times a day (ARAVIND fitz 253).
  - Atropine 1% (or homatropine 2%) three times a day (BAIDYA fitz 193), over "atropine eye ointment 4%" (ARAVIND fitz 253).
  - Anti-VEGF 2–3 days before surgery (BAIDYA fitz 194), over "usually 1 week, can be 14–48 h" (NAMRATA fitz 212) and "24–78 h" (ARAVIND fitz 254).
  - Prostaglandin analogues "contraindicated" (BAIDYA fitz 193), over "relative contraindication" (ARAVIND fitz 250).
  - Angle-closure stage, new vessels "less apparent" (NAMRATA fitz 211 table and fitz 212 differential diagnosis), over "florid NVI/NVA" (ARAVIND fitz 248). Note that NAMRATA's own Q7 (fitz 214) says "heavy neovascularization".
- **Other key values verified:**
  - Bevacizumab 1.25 mg/0.05 mL: BAIDYA fitz 265; NAMRATA fitz 213; ARAVIND fitz 252, 254. Regression within 24–48 h: NAMRATA fitz 212.
  - Timolol 0.5% twice daily, dorzolamide 2% three times a day, acetazolamide 250 mg: BAIDYA fitz 171–172 drug table (acetazolamide 2–4 times daily; the card's "twice daily" is ARAVIND fitz 253 and lies inside that range). Brimonidine 0.2% three times a day: ARAVIND fitz 252 only; BAIDYA's table has no brimonidine row.
  - IOP threshold of 40 mm Hg: ARAVIND fitz 252–253.
  - NVA without NVI in 12%: NAMRATA fitz 213. A third of rubeotic eyes have diabetic retinopathy: NAMRATA fitz 213.
  - Wand's classification: ARAVIND fitz 248 only (not in BAIDYA or NAMRATA).
  - "100-day glaucoma": NAMRATA fitz 214 (after 3 months) and fitz 240 (NVI usually at 2–4 months); ARAVIND fitz 249 (3–5 months, range 2 months to 2 years). These are compatible.
  - Antimetabolites MMC 0.2–0.4 mg/mL for 2 min and 5-FU 50 mg/mL for 5 min: ARAVIND fitz 252–253 (4.9), repeated at fitz 258 (4.10). BAIDYA and NAMRATA give no doses.

## 4. Confirm with your seniors
1. **PRP settings in NVG.** The card follows the newest book: 500 µm, 0.1 s, mild white burns one burn-width apart, 1200–1600 burns in 3–4 sittings. The older FAQ book gives a heavier set for NVG (1800–2200 burns, moderately intense, half a burn-width apart, in 2–3 sittings over 3–6 weeks). The AIIMS book says about 2000–2500 spots in 2–3 sittings. Ask which numbers your examiners expect for NVG.
2. **Timing of anti-VEGF before surgery.** The card says 2–3 days (newest book). The other books say usually 1 week (can be 14–48 hours), and 24–78 hours.
3. **Mannitol dose.** The card says 20%, 1–2 g/kg over 20–30 minutes. One book's NVG chapter prints "100 mL … 2 g/kg", which does not add up. Another book gives 1.5 g/kg (200 mL) over 30 minutes. Confirm the dose your department uses.
4. **Why ectropion uveae occurs.** The card follows two books: the contracting fibrovascular membrane pulls the posterior pigment layer round the pupil margin. The newest book links it to "repeated uveitic attacks". Confirm which viva answer is preferred.
5. **Antimetabolite strength for trabeculectomy.** This card gives 5-fluorouracil 50 mg/mL for 5 min and mitomycin C 0.2–0.4 mg/mL for 2 min (the book's NVG chapter). The trabeculectomy card (G2) gives 30 mg/mL and 0.2–0.5 mg/mL for 1–5 min (the same book's trabeculectomy chapter). Confirm the strengths your department uses.
