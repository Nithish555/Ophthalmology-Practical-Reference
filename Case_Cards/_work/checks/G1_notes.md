## Claims ledger

### At a glance
- POAG = chronic progressive optic neuropathy, open normal-appearing angle, diminished outflow — NAMRATA fitz 189; BAIDYA fitz 169
- Usually asymptomatic, found on routine examination or follow-up — BAIDYA fitz 166–167; NAMRATA fitz 189–190
- Commonly kept as a long case — NAMRATA fitz 189
- IOP to be recorded with method (GAT), time and CCT — BAIDYA fitz 168; NAMRATA fitz 191
- Badge "kept 6 times, one with pseudoexfoliation glaucoma" — CARDS_GLAUCOMA.md / caller brief (not a packet fact)

### History
- Chief complaint and HOPI house wording ("apparently normal… gradual… progressive… not associated with pain/headache… 1 drop at bedtime") — department sheet, proforma PDF p.7 (house format)
- Blurring at a particular time of day (early morning, afternoon) with heaviness; depends on IOP peak — BAIDYA fitz 166; NAMRATA fitz 190
- Frequent change of near glasses — BAIDYA fitz 166; why: pressure on zonules and ciliary body impairs accommodation; field loss confused with poor vision — ARAVIND fitz 22
- Night blindness: defective dark adaptation in open-angle glaucoma; miotics accentuate it — ARAVIND fitz 22
- Old records: baseline IOP, previous fields, OCT RNFL, GDx; compliance — BAIDYA fitz 167; NAMRATA fitz 190
- Haloes from corneal oedema; acute attack with pain, redness, headache, nausea and vomiting = angle closure — NAMRATA fitz 196; BAIDYA fitz 172
- Trauma → angle-recession glaucoma — BAIDYA fitz 167; NAMRATA fitz 190
- Steroids in any form (drops, nasal spray, systemic) → steroid-induced glaucoma — NAMRATA fitz 190; BAIDYA fitz 167
- Sudden loss of vision in a glaucoma patient: acute IOP rise with corneal oedema, central retinal vein occlusion, snuff-out after trabeculectomy — ARAVIND fitz 23
- Systemic risk: diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disease, cardiovascular disease, migraine, Raynaud's — BAIDYA fitz 167; NAMRATA fitz 190
- Vascular/ischaemic factors especially significant in NTG — NAMRATA fitz 195; BAIDYA fitz 172
- Stroke, acute blood loss (haemodynamic crisis) worsen optic nerve damage — NAMRATA fitz 190
- Previous acute optic insult (hypovolaemic shock, head injury) is a differential of NTG — BAIDYA fitz 174
- Head injury, stroke (CVA), peripheral vascular disease, migraine, Raynaud's, sulpha and other drug allergy, steroid use — seniors' checklist, proforma PDF p.7 (house format)
- Beta-blockers contraindicated in congestive cardiac failure, 2nd/3rd degree heart block, bradycardia, asthma, COPD — ARAVIND fitz 276; NAMRATA fitz 193; asthma — TYPED FORMAT fitz 0
- CAIs avoided in sulpha allergy, renal failure, renal transplant, chronic liver disease — ARAVIND fitz 281
- Hypersensitivity to antiglaucoma drugs and sulpha allergy to be asked — NAMRATA fitz 190; BAIDYA fitz 167
- Refractive surgery reduces CCT → IOP falsely low — NAMRATA fitz 196
- Previous surgery list (cataract, trabeculectomy, keratoplasty, vitreoretinal surgery, PI) — BAIDYA fitz 167; NAMRATA fitz 190
- Topical medication for over 3 years → mitomycin C essential at trabeculectomy — BAIDYA fitz 179
- Treatment-history format "E/D timolol 0.5% BD" — CARD_SPEC §5 (house format); timolol 0.5% twice daily — BAIDYA fitz 171; ARAVIND fitz 277
- Family history: risk 4-fold in siblings, 2-fold in offspring — BAIDYA fitz 167

### Special tests
- Gonioscopy lowers the applanation reading; dilatation raises IOP 4–5 mm Hg — NAMRATA fitz 195
- GAT accurate at CCT 520 µm — ARAVIND fitz 243
- Shaffer grades: 4 = 35–45°, 3 = 25–35°, 2 = 10–25°, 1 = 0–10°, 0 = 0° — TYPED FORMAT fitz 1
- Gonio cross with grade and deepest structure — proforma PDF p.9; lastyear.md (house format)
- Disc with +90 D (or +78 D) at the slit lamp — BAIDYA fitz 168; NAMRATA fitz 191
- Distant direct then direct ophthalmoscopy — TYPED FORMAT fitz 1; department fundus formula (CARD_SPEC §5)
- ISNT rule: rim inferior > superior > nasal > temporal — NAMRATA fitz 194; ARAVIND fitz 242; rule violated in glaucoma — BAIDYA fitz 169
- RNFL defects with green filter / red-free light — BAIDYA fitz 169; NAMRATA fitz 192
- Laminar dot sign — BAIDYA fitz 168; NAMRATA fitz 191; ARAVIND fitz 22
- Confrontation: 60 cm, patient's right eye fixes examiner's left, other eyes closed, finger from periphery midway — BAIDYA fitz 176

### Summary, diagnosis, differentials
- Department diagnosis wording — proforma PDF p.9
- "Pseudoexfoliation glaucoma / syndrome"; unilateral cupping in PXF — BAIDYA fitz 181; NAMRATA fitz 189; "severe stage" — BAIDYA fitz 178
- OHT: IOP > 21 mm Hg on 2 consecutive readings; normal disc, rim, field; open angle — BAIDYA fitz 169; NAMRATA fitz 192
- NTG: IOP < 22 mm Hg on diurnal curve; more disc haemorrhages, focal notching, PPA — BAIDYA fitz 173–174; ARAVIND fitz 240
- PXF glaucoma: exfoliation material; higher IOP, greater 24-hour fluctuation — NAMRATA fitz 192
- Pigmentary glaucoma: age 20–30; Krukenberg spindle; mid-peripheral transillumination defects; wide-open angle — NAMRATA fitz 192; BAIDYA fitz 169
- Steroid-induced: steroid intake; posterior subcapsular cataract — NAMRATA fitz 192
- Angle recession: trauma; recession on gonioscopy; usually unilateral — NAMRATA fitz 192

### Definition, risk factors, classification, pathogenesis
- Glaucoma definition (irreversible optic nerve damage, corresponding field defects, with or without high IOP) — BAIDYA fitz 169
- Adult onset > 40 years; juvenile 3–40 years — NAMRATA fitz 189
- Normal IOP 10–21 mm Hg — BAIDYA fitz 169
- Risk factors: high IOP, myopia, thin CCT, diastolic perfusion pressure < 55 mm Hg, age (prevalence roughly doubles each decade over 40), Black race, first-degree relatives, no gender predisposition — ARAVIND fitz 239; thin CCT = higher risk of POAG — BAIDYA fitz 168
- Grading of glaucomatous optic nerve head damage (mild: minimal cupping, nasal step or paracentral scotoma; moderate: NRR thinning, arcuate scotoma; severe: marked cupping, defects within central 5°; end-stage: gross cupping, small residual field) — BAIDYA fitz 178
- MD cut-offs −6 dB and −12 dB — BAIDYA fitz 178; ARAVIND fitz 225
- Z values: suspect (low and high risk) 0, early 1, moderate 3, severe 5, end-stage 7 — ARAVIND fitz 24–25 (checked on the page image)
- Maximum outflow resistance at juxtacanalicular meshwork — ARAVIND fitz 240; BAIDYA fitz 170
- Raised IOP → backward bowing of lamina cribrosa → axoplasmic transport blocked → ganglion cell death — NAMRATA fitz 195; apoptosis — ARAVIND fitz 240
- Nocturnal hypotension, vasospasm, blood loss — BAIDYA fitz 172; NAMRATA fitz 195
- Poles have larger laminar pores — ARAVIND fitz 242; BAIDYA fitz 176
- Cupping starts in lower temporal quadrant → upper arcuate scotoma — ARAVIND fitz 241
- Myocilin chromosome 1; optineurin chromosome 10 — ARAVIND fitz 239; NAMRATA fitz 195

### Investigations
- Diurnal variation every 3 hours for 24 hours; difference ≥ 8 mm Hg significant; helps timing of drugs — NAMRATA fitz 192
- Normal diurnal variation 3–6 mm Hg — BAIDYA fitz 170
- Phasing: diagnosis, progression despite good control, efficacy, NTG versus POAG — ARAVIND fitz 239
- Pachymetry corrects IOP — NAMRATA fitz 192; thin < 500 µm, thick > 570 µm — NAMRATA fitz 196; thin reads falsely low, thick falsely high — ARAVIND fitz 243
- Humphrey 24-2 / 30-2; 10-2 when fixation threatened — ARAVIND fitz 224
- Glaucomatous defects follow the horizontal meridian — ARAVIND fitz 222; paracentral, arcuate (Bjerrum), nasal step — ARAVIND fitz 221–222
- OCT, GDx, HRT, SWAP as pre-perimetric tools — NAMRATA fitz 195; ARAVIND fitz 243
- RNFL interocular difference > 9 µm — BAIDYA fitz 210
- Double hump lost; superior and inferior first; 7 o'clock RE, 5 o'clock LE — BAIDYA fitz 212
- Macular GCC; useful in early disease with unreliable fields and in advanced disease (floor effect) — BAIDYA fitz 209

### Viva-only facts
- 25–30% ganglion cell loss before white-on-white field loss — ARAVIND fitz 243
- Disc haemorrhage: active disease, more frequent in NTG, precedes field defects by months, inferotemporal, flame-shaped — ARAVIND fitz 242; BAIDYA fitz 169
- Bayoneting = double angulation — BAIDYA fitz 169; nasalisation — ARAVIND fitz 242; baring of circumlinear vessels — ARAVIND fitz 242; BAIDYA fitz 169
- Beta zone next to disc with visible sclera — ARAVIND fitz 242
- Vertical CDR more important (early rim loss at poles); normal cup horizontally oval — NAMRATA fitz 195; ARAVIND fitz 242
- NTG a disease of exclusion — ARAVIND fitz 240
- Suspect other lesion: pallor more than cupping, dyschromatopsia, field respecting vertical meridian → neuroimaging — ARAVIND fitz 244; BAIDYA fitz 175
- PG peak 10–14 hours; bedtime lessens vascular-dilatation symptoms — ARAVIND fitz 281
- Timolol morning: blunts early-morning rise, avoids hypotension in sleep — ARAVIND fitz 277, 284
- Timolol systemic: bradycardia, hypotension, bronchospasm, depression — ARAVIND fitz 277

## Disagreements (and what the card uses)
- Family risk: BAIDYA fitz 167 "4-fold siblings, 2-fold offspring" vs ARAVIND fitz 239 "siblings 10%, offspring 4%". Card: Baidya.
- CDR asymmetry: BAIDYA fitz 168 "≥ 0.2" vs BAIDYA fitz 175 and NAMRATA fitz 191, 194 "> 0.2". Card: "> 0.2" (Baidya is inconsistent with itself; Namrata agrees with Baidya's FAQ).
- Normal IOP: BAIDYA fitz 169 "10–21" vs BAIDYA fitz 174 "12–22". NTG ceiling: BAIDYA fitz 173–174 "< 22" vs ARAVIND fitz 240 "< 21". Card: 10–21 normal; NTG < 22 (Baidya).
- CCT and progression: ARAVIND fitz 239 (thin CCT raises risk of progression) vs NAMRATA fitz 196 (CCT does not predict progression). Card says only "thin CCT is a risk factor" (BAIDYA fitz 168: higher risk for POAG).
- Nocturnal blood pressure: NAMRATA fitz 195 "nocturnal hypertension" vs BAIDYA fitz 172 "systemic nocturnal hypotension". Card: hypotension (Namrata looks like a typo).
- First-line drug: BAIDYA fitz 170 and ARAVIND fitz 25 PG first; NAMRATA fitz 192 "beta-blockers and PG often first choice; one or two drugs". Card: PG (Baidya).
- Timolol frequency: BAIDYA fitz 171 twice daily; NAMRATA fitz 193 "OD or BD". Card: twice daily.
- Brimonidine: NAMRATA fitz 193 0.1/0.15%; ARAVIND fitz 278 0.2% tartrate, 0.15% purite; Baidya silent. Card lists all three.
- Acetazolamide: BAIDYA fitz 172 250 mg 2–4 times daily vs ARAVIND fitz 280 250 mg 6-hourly or 500 mg SR twice daily. Card: Baidya.
- Pilocarpine: BAIDYA fitz 170 0.5–4%, 2–4 times daily vs NAMRATA fitz 193 0.5/1/2%, usually QID. Card: Baidya.
- Brinzolamide: ARAVIND fitz 280 1% twice daily vs NAMRATA fitz 193 CAI class "TID sometimes BD". Left off the card.
- Unoprostone: NAMRATA 0.15% vs ARAVIND 0.12%. Left off. "Metoprolol 0.3%/0.6%" (BAIDYA fitz 172, probably metipranolol) vs ARAVIND fitz 277 metipranolol 0.1%, 0.3%. Left off.
- Severity "mild": BAIDYA fitz 178 (nasal step or paracentral scotoma, MD better than −6) vs NAMRATA fitz 195 (AAO: normal SAP field). Card staging uses Baidya; Namrata's percentage targets kept as "mild / advanced damage".

- Field extent (not on card): BAIDYA fitz 176 (60 N, 107 T, 70 up, 80 down) vs ARAVIND fitz 220 (60 up, 75 down, 60 N, 120 T).
- Row order: CARD_SPEC template puts IOP after the lens; the department's POAG sheet (proforma p.9) puts "Tn by GAT" right after vision and EOM. Card follows the department sheet.
- Aravind page labels: the packet header maps the model case sheet (fitz 20–26) to "printed p.-6 to 0"; the pages themselves are numbered 661–667. @readmore uses 661–667.
