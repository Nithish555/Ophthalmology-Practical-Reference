### What you must know
- Definition (alloplastic tube leading to a large equatorial reservoir that drains aqueous from the AC): ARAVIND fitz 319 (Q1)
- Parts: ridge where the tube inserts (separates the capsule from the posterior tube orifice so it does not block it; directs flow onto the explant's upper surface): fitz 319 (Q5 iv). Valve: fitz 321 (Q12). Capsule: fitz 319 (Q7), fitz 323 (Q16 iii). Patch graft: fitz 325 (Q28)
- Common features (materials fibroblasts cannot firmly adhere to; equatorial explant; similar tube diameter): ARAVIND fitz 319 (Q5 i–iii)
- Seton (non-lumened, flow along the surface through the subconjunctival space), shunt (hollow, bidirectional), valve (unidirectional, needs an activating pressure): ARAVIND fitz 319 (Q2–4)
- Molteno: first, non-valved, polypropylene, circular plate, tube outer diameter 0.63 mm and inner 0.3 mm, single, double or triple plate: ARAVIND fitz 321 (Q9)
- Baerveldt: non-valved, silicone: ARAVIND fitz 321 (Q10)
- Ahmed: oval polypropylene plate 184 mm² (double plate 364 mm²), silicone tube, 2 layers of silicone elastomer membrane, one-way valve by the Venturi effect (Bernoulli's principle), outflow only above 8–10 mm Hg, fully open at high pressure and narrowing as the pressure falls: ARAVIND fitz 321 (Q12)
- Non-valved sizing (larger for younger, healthier eyes; smaller for older, sicker eyes): ARAVIND fitz 325 (Q33)
- Drainage capacity ∝ capsule surface area; resistance ∝ capsule thickness: ARAVIND fitz 319 (Q7)
- Devices with valves or flow restrictors work at once (one-stage); non-valved devices wait for the capsule: ARAVIND fitz 323 (Q16 ii, iii)
- GAT high: blocked tube (fitz 327 xi), thick capsule (v), hypertensive phase (vii). GAT low: hypotony (fitz 326 early iii)
- Corneal oedema: take the IOP as higher than read; measure by GAT after the oedema subsides: BAIDYA fitz 193
- +90 D stereoscopic disc view: BAIDYA fitz 193. Indirect ophthalmoscopy: NAMRATA fitz 204 (for the periphery and detachment, SWS context); ARAVIND fitz 324 (Q21, indirect ophthalmoscope)
- Indications list (uveitic, neovascular, congenital, refractory juvenile, multiple failed trabeculectomy, extensive conjunctival scarring, post-PKP, aniridia, post-traumatic, failed primary glaucomas): ARAVIND fitz 320 (Q8); failed filtering surgery with antimetabolites, conjunctiva too scarred for filtering surgery: NAMRATA fitz 194
- Pre-operative checks (useful vision, lid position, scleral exposure, tear film, blepharitis, peripheral corneal clarity, pseudophakia, shallow AC, post-PKP and endothelial function → pars plana tube, vitreous strands may occlude the tip, cataract for combined surgery, PAS to be avoided, NVA treated first): ARAVIND fitz 322 (Q14)
- No useful vision → cyclodestruction: BAIDYA fitz 189, 194; NAMRATA fitz 204, 213
- Stop pilocarpine or echothiophate (less postoperative inflammation) and warfarin; continue other drops: ARAVIND fitz 322 (Q15)
- Step 1, limbus-based or fornix-based flap: ARAVIND fitz 323 (Q16 i)
- Step 2, superotemporal: ARAVIND fitz 323 (Q20 i). Next inferonasal: BAIDYA fitz 187. Superonasal → vertical strabismus (pseudo-Brown's syndrome) and impact on the optic nerve: ARAVIND fitz 324 (Q23)

## Disagreements
- **Valved vs non-valved efficacy**: ARAVIND fitz 325 (Q32) says safety and efficacy are not clearly different and "appear to be equal". BAIDYA fitz 189 says the Ahmed valve is superior to the Molteno for long-term control, but only in Sturge–Weber syndrome. These are different settings, not a direct conflict, and Baidya makes no general claim. The card keeps Aravind's general statement. The SWS point was cut for space (see below).
- **Device vs trabeculectomy outcome**: ARAVIND fitz 326 (Q35) says the percentage IOP fall is smaller with a device. NAMRATA fitz 213 says devices in NVG "have shown comparable results to trabeculectomy". These are different measures in different diseases. The card states Aravind's point (POAG context) and leaves out Namrata's NVG comparison.
- **Anti-VEGF timing before surgery in NVG**: BAIDYA fitz 194 says 2–3 days before PPV + PRP + filtration or valve. NAMRATA fitz 212 says usually 1 week before PRP or surgery, and can be 14–48 hours. The card uses **Baidya (2–3 days)**, the newer book.
- **Cyclodestruction complications**: BAIDYA fitz 187 lists hypotony and retinal detachment; BAIDYA fitz 189 lists hypotony and phthisis bulbi. NAMRATA fitz 213 adds that repeat treatment may be needed. There is no conflict; the card combines them.
- **Second site for the plate**: ARAVIND fitz 323 gives superotemporal only. BAIDYA fitz 187 gives superotemporal, then inferonasal. These are complementary; the card gives both.
- **Pars plana tube indications**: ARAVIND fitz 324 (Q26) says "aphakia and PCIOL eyes, post-PKP, shallow AC, decreased endothelial function". Q14 (fitz 322) lists PCIOL, shallow AC, post-PKP and endothelial function without aphakia. They are consistent; the viva answer uses the Q26 list.
- **Aravind internal slips (not on the card)**: Q17 calls "8-0 polyglactin or 10-0 prolene" absorbable, but prolene is not absorbable, so the card does not call the pair absorbable. Q26 "sharp level" is read as "sharp bevel". The Q34 Schocket "10° prolene" is presumably 10-0; that device is not on the card.

## Format decisions
- **Differential = "which refractory secondary glaucoma could this be?"**: in an eye with a device, the diagnosis challenge is the underlying glaucoma. The causes of raised IOP with a device (tie, block, thick capsule, hypertensive phase) sit in the Investigations GAT row and the complications table.
- **Tube given its own row** in the RE | LE table, between anterior chamber and iris. "Pupil, lens, fields" are merged into one master-proforma row to save words.
- **Special tests** say "Record: as in the table" to avoid repeating the table's examples. Gonioscopy is recorded by the `:::gonio` cross (all III SS; the caption notes the tube tip superotemporally).
- **No short-case version**: the badge is "Long case" (brief).
- **Viva at 20 questions**: the brief's minimum, set by the word budget. The first group has 5 Aravind-style "Why did you…" questions.
- **Earlier G3.md overwritten**: the older draft in drafts/ (07:25) had claims this packet does not support: Baerveldt fenestrations and a 0.64 mm tube, the GDD principle and absorption route, an erosion stent, wider patch-graft benefits, and "hypertensive phase is transient" stated as fact.
