## Claims ledger

### What it is
- Static perimetry: a stationary stimulus; a dim light made gradually brighter until it is seen — BAIDYA fitz 195; ARAVIND fitz 220 (Q5, "e.g. Humphrey's perimetry")
- Threshold = intensity seen 50% of the time (seen 50 of 100 presentations) — BAIDYA fitz 196; ARAVIND fitz 223 (Q29)
- Automated perimetry is the current gold standard in the diagnosis and management of glaucoma — BAIDYA fitz 195
- Goldmann size III stimulus for all routine tests; colour white — BAIDYA fitz 199 (d, e)
- Background 31.5 asb — BAIDYA fitz 198 (c); ARAVIND fitz 226 (Q42, Goldmann bowl)
- 0 dB = 10,000 asb (maximum HFA intensity); 40 dB = 1 asb (least intensity) — BAIDYA fitz 197
- Higher dB = more sensitive (retinal sensitivity directly proportional to dB) — BAIDYA fitz 197, 200
- 24-2: 6° spacing, 54 points; 30-2: 6°, 76 points; 10-2: 2°, 68 points, advanced glaucoma — BAIDYA fitz 199 (table); ARAVIND fitz 224 (Q32 "6 degree grid"; Q34 30-2 = 76 points, 6° apart)
- SITA: shorter testing time, "little or no detriment to testing accuracy" — BAIDYA fitz 199–200; takes 50% of the regular threshold strategy time — ARAVIND fitz 224 (Q31)

### Reading order
- Read reliability indices before making a diagnosis; identify the pattern on the pattern deviation plot — IMAGING fitz 25
- 8 zones, their numbers and order (1 patient data, 2 test conditions, 3 reliability indices, 4 grey scale, 5 raw data, 6 total deviation plot, which includes pattern deviation and VFI, 7 GHT, 8 global indices) — BAIDYA fitz 198 (list), 198–206 (text)
- Where each zone sits (header strip; top-left reliability block; top centre and top right test-condition blocks; raw numbers upper left, grey scale upper right; total deviation lower left and pattern deviation lower right, numbers above and symbols below; GHT in the right column with MD/PSD under it) — layout of fields_viva p01–p04 and IMAGING fitz 24 (slide image). This is a layout observation, not a clinical claim
- Zone 1: the correct age is essential, because raw data are compared with age-matched normal values — BAIDYA fitz 198
- Zone 2: test conditions are printed at the top; pupil normally >2.5 mm; <2 mm gives generalised depression, so dilate if <2 mm; refractive error must be corrected or there is generalised depression — BAIDYA fitz 198 (a, b)
- Fixation losses: dots flashed in the physiological blind spot, seen = fixation loss; reliable test below 20% — BAIDYA fitz 200; "below 20%" — IMAGING fitz 24 (slide image)
- "8/19 = 42%, unreliable": arithmetic on the fraction printed on a practice printout (fields_viva p03), judged by the 20% rule above
- False positives >15% = unreliable; "trigger happy"; white scotoma on the grey scale — BAIDYA fitz 200, fitz 201 (Fig. 5.4)
- False negatives >33% = unreliable; fatigue, inattention or advanced glaucomatous damage; clover-leaf pattern on the grey scale — BAIDYA fitz 200, fitz 201 (Fig. 5.5); ARAVIND fitz 223 (Q28, >33%)
- Foveal threshold should be explained by the visual acuity; low foveal threshold with good acuity → check optical correction — BAIDYA fitz 198 (a), 200 (d); IMAGING fitz 24 ("foveal threshold (corresponding to the VA)")
- Grey scale: graphical form of the raw dB values, no statistical or normative analysis — BAIDYA fitz 200 (4)
- Raw data: measured threshold in dB at each point; 0 = absolute scotoma — BAIDYA fitz 200 (5)
- Total deviation: measured value minus the age-matched normal; negative = below normal — BAIDYA fitz 203 (a); shows both diffuse and localised depression — IMAGING fitz 24 (slide image)
- Pattern deviation: the dB value that brings the 7th best TDNP point to 0 is added to all points; reveals focal defects masked by generalised depression — BAIDYA fitz 204 (i)
- GHT: five sectors above the horizontal raphe compared with their mirror images below; results: within normal limits, borderline (p<3%), outside normal limits (p<1%), general reduction in sensitivity, abnormally high sensitivity — BAIDYA fitz 204–205; ARAVIND fitz 225 (Q37)
- MD: overall deviation; normal "typically within 0 dB to 2 dB"; more negative as the field worsens — BAIDYA fitz 205–206
- PSD: focal defects; high = focal loss; low = no loss or diffuse loss — BAIDYA fitz 206 (b); ARAVIND fitz 225 (Q36)
- VFI: percent of a normal, age-adjusted field; developed from PDNP, so not affected by cataract — BAIDYA fitz 204 (ii)
- Conclude with the diagnosis and the probable nerve fibre layer / optic nerve head defect — IMAGING fitz 25
- "Superior defect: inferior pole" is INDIRECT support. BAIDYA fitz 196 says the inferior retina's lower sensitivity produces superior baring of the blind spot, so the inferior retina serves the superior field. ARAVIND fitz 221 (Q14) says arcuate loss, especially in the superior half, correlates with the predilection of the poles. No packet sentence states the rule outright.

### Findings to name
- Defects first appear in Bjerrum's area, 10–25° from fixation, and correlate with optic disc changes — BAIDYA fitz 196, 197
- Glaucomatous defects follow the horizontal meridian, are relative and incongruous; neurological defects follow the vertical meridian, are absolute and mostly congruous — ARAVIND fitz 222 (Q21)
- Order of appearance: generalised depression → baring of the blind spot → paracentral → Seidel's → arcuate → ring (double arcuate) → nasal (Roenne) step → tubular vision with temporal island — BAIDYA fitz 197 (items 1–8); ARAVIND fitz 221 (Q11: same sequence without baring, plus "progressive contraction")
- Generalised depression: earliest, "not so important in diagnosis" — BAIDYA fitz 197 (1)
- Baring of the blind spot: early, "very nonspecific" — BAIDYA fitz 197 (2)
- Paracentral scotoma: earliest clinically significant defect — ARAVIND fitz 222 (Q16); most commonly superonasal, terminates at the horizontal midline — BAIDYA fitz 197 (3)
- Seidel's scotoma: paracentral scotoma joins the blind spot, sickle-shaped — BAIDYA fitz 197 (4); ARAVIND fitz 222 (Q17)
- Arcuate (Bjerrum) scotoma: from the blind spot, arching above or below fixation to the horizontal median raphe — ARAVIND fitz 221 (Q14); BAIDYA fitz 197 (5)
- Ring (double arcuate) scotoma — BAIDYA fitz 197 (6); ARAVIND fitz 222 (Q18)
- Nasal (Roenne) step: sensitivity differs above and below the horizontal midline in the nasal field — BAIDYA fitz 197 (7); a sharp right-angled defect at the horizontal meridian — ARAVIND fitz 222 (Q19)
- Advanced: tubular vision and a temporal island — BAIDYA fitz 197 (8)
- Anderson and Patella criteria: a cluster of 3 or more contiguous non-edge points on the 30-2 pattern deviation plot, all depressed at p<5% and one at p<1%; "PSD <5%"; GHT outside normal limits; "at least in two consecutive tests" — BAIDYA fitz 206. "CPSD at P<5% level", "two consecutive occasions" — ARAVIND fitz 225–226 (Q40). "Arcuate area", "PSD/CPSD p<5%", "at least 2 reliable fields" — IMAGING fitz 25

### Grading or classification
- MD early <−6 dB, moderate −6 to −12 dB, severe >−12 dB; CPSD depressed to p<5% at all stages; pattern deviation points depressed below p<5%: <18 (25%) / <37 (50%) / >37 (>50%); below p<1%: <10 / <20 / >20; GHT outside normal limits at all stages; central 5°: no point <15 dB / one hemifield may have a point <15 dB / both hemifields have points <15 dB — ARAVIND fitz 225 (Q39)
- MD wording "−6 dB or better", "−6 to −12 dB", "−12 dB or worse"; central 10° of fixation not involved (mild, moderate) or involved (severe) — BAIDYA fitz 207 (Fig. 5.12b), 208 (Fig. 5.12c, d)

### What each finding leads to (management)
- Unreliable → do not diagnose; repeat: the criteria must be shown on at least 2 reliable fields — IMAGING fitz 25; "two consecutive tests" — BAIDYA fitz 206. "Re-instruct" is my wording.
- Uniform generalised depression (cataract, media opacity, small pupil, uncorrected refractive error) leaves the pattern deviation probability plot normal — BAIDYA fitz 204 (iii); correct refraction and dilate a pupil under 2 mm — BAIDYA fitz 198
- Criteria met and matching the disc → glaucomatous field defect — BAIDYA fitz 197 (defects correlate with optic disc changes), fitz 206 (criteria); IMAGING fitz 25
- Advanced loss threatening fixation → serial 10-2 — ARAVIND fitz 224 (Q32); 10-2 for advanced glaucoma — BAIDYA fitz 199 (table); size V in advanced glaucoma to know the status of the macular split — BAIDYA fitz 199 (d); severe = involving central 10° — BAIDYA fitz 208
- Serial fields: worsening MD/VFI, expanding or deepening scotomas, new localised defects — IMAGING fitz 25; change (trend) analysis, and glaucoma progression analysis comparing baseline with follow-up PDNP — BAIDYA fitz 206
- "Review treatment" is only general support: perimetry is the gold standard in glaucoma management (BAIDYA fitz 195). The packet gives no specific step after progression (see Omitted).
- Does not fit: vertical meridian, absolute, congruous (neurological) — ARAVIND fitz 222 (Q21). Test-related causes and other diseases: poor reliability, uncorrected prescription, eyelid obstruction, small pupils, fatigue, dry eye; retinal disease, non-glaucomatous optic neuropathy, neurological disease, media opacity — IMAGING fitz 25

### Viva
- Why PD, not TD — BAIDYA fitz 203 (c), 204 (i)
- Effect of cataract: TD depressed — BAIDYA fitz 203 (b); PD normal in uniform generalised defect — fitz 204 (iii); VFI unaffected — fitz 204 (ii); PSD low in diffuse loss — fitz 206 (b). "MD depressed" is inferred from MD being the average of the TD values (fitz 205–206).
- p<2% means fewer than 2% of normal people would have such low sensitivity at that point; the probability plot does not show depth — BAIDYA fitz 203 (b)
- Glaucomatous versus neurological — ARAVIND fitz 222 (Q21)
- Other causes of an arcuate scotoma (myopia with peripapillary atrophy, drusen, ischaemic optic neuropathy, pituitary adenoma) — ARAVIND fitz 221–222 (Q15)
- Progression: reproducible depression >7 dB within a scotoma (deepening), >9 dB adjacent (enlargement), >11 dB at a previously normal point (new) — ARAVIND fitz 225 (Q38)
- False positives 20% → unreliable (>15%), trigger-happy, white scotomas — BAIDYA fitz 200; 20% is the value on practice printout fields_viva p04
- Earlier detection: short-wavelength automated perimetry (blue-on-yellow), more sensitive in early glaucoma — ARAVIND fitz 222–223 (Q23); frequency-doubling technology, most sensitive for early glaucomatous loss — ARAVIND fitz 223 (Q25)

## Disagreements
- **Reliability cut-offs.**
  - False positives: BAIDYA fitz 200 says >15% is unreliable; ARAVIND fitz 223 (Q27) says >33%; IMAGING fitz 24 says "below 20%".
  - False negatives: BAIDYA and ARAVIND say >33%; IMAGING fitz 24 says "below 20%".
  - Fixation losses: BAIDYA and IMAGING agree on <20%.
  - The card uses Baidya: fixation losses <20%, false positives >15%, false negatives >33%.
- **Bjerrum's area.** BAIDYA fitz 196–197 gives 10–25°; ARAVIND fitz 221 (Q13) gives 10–20°. The card uses 10–25°. Cross-card flag: card G1's viva answer says "Bjerrum's area 10–20°" (Aravind), so G1 should be aligned to 10–25° under the newer-book rule.
- **Zone scheme.**
  - BAIDYA fitz 198 has 8 zones: grey scale is zone 4 before raw data (5), and GHT is zone 7 before global indices (8). Pattern deviation and VFI sit inside zone 6.
  - IMAGING fitz 24–25 has 7 zones: 1 patient and test data, 2 reliability, 3 raw data and grey scale, 4 total deviation, 5 pattern deviation, 6 global indices (MD, PSD, VFI), 7 GHT.
  - The brief's suggested order (raw data before grey scale, global indices before GHT) matches neither.
  - The card uses Baidya's numbers and order, as the brief asked for the packet's zone numbers. On the printout, GHT is printed above MD/PSD, so reading zone 7 before zone 8 also runs top to bottom.
- **MD staging notation.** ARAVIND fitz 225 prints "<−6 dB / −6 dB — −12 dB / >−12 dB", which is ambiguous about sign. BAIDYA fitz 207–208 says "−6 dB or better / between −6 and −12 dB / −12 dB or worse". The card uses Baidya's wording.
- **Anderson criteria wording.**
  - BAIDYA: "3 or more contiguous non-edge points ... 30-2 ... pattern deviation plot", "PSD <5%", "at least in two consecutive tests", named "Anderson and Patella criteria".
  - ARAVIND: "three or more nonedge points in a location typical of glaucoma", "CPSD at P<5%", "two consecutive occasions", named "Anderson's criteria".
  - IMAGING: "3 or more congruous non-edge points in arcuate area on 30-2", "PSD/CPSD p<5%", "at least 2 reliable fields".
  - The card uses Baidya's cluster wording and name, "PSD or CPSD p<5%" (reading Baidya's "PSD <5%" as a probability level, as the other two state it), and "at least 2 consecutive reliable fields".
- **GHT "outside normal limits".** BAIDYA fitz 205 gives p<1%. IMAGING fitz 24 gives "all cluster points differ p<1%, or 1 cluster pair differs p<0.5%" and "borderline: hemifield differs p<3%". These do not conflict; the card gives Baidya's single figures.
- **Background luminance.** BAIDYA fitz 198 gives 31.5 asb. ARAVIND fitz 220 (Q6, perimetry in general) says "between 4 and 31 apostilbs"; ARAVIND fitz 226 (Q42, Goldmann) gives 31.5 asb. The card uses 31.5 asb (it is also printed on every practice printout).
- **Pupil.** BAIDYA fitz 198 says normally >2.5 mm, dilate if <2 mm. ARAVIND fitz 220 (Q6) says keep it constant, 2–4 mm. The card uses Baidya.
- **Earliest defect.** BAIDYA calls generalised depression the earliest; ARAVIND calls paracentral scotoma the earliest clinically significant. These are compatible, and the card gives both.
- **Gaze tracking.** IMAGING fitz 24 lists "flat line: no movement" and also "flat line: eye closure", which contradict each other. Not used.
- Not on the card: the normal temporal field is 100° in BAIDYA fitz 196 and 120° in ARAVIND fitz 220.

## Format decisions
- Zone steps are flat numbered lines. The dialect defines nesting only under bullets, and no other draft nests under a numbered step.
- Each step reads "Zone N, name (where on the printout)". The positions come from the department's own HFA II printouts.
- Zone 6 is labelled "deviation plots" so that it covers both total and pattern deviation, which Baidya puts in the same zone.
- 8 viva questions (the template minimum), because of the word cap.
- No cross-reference to card G1 (dropped for words). The previous G10.md draft in this folder (no notes file) was replaced. Its "normal MD 0 to −2 dB" changed the packet's sign, so this card uses the packet's own "0 to 2 dB".
