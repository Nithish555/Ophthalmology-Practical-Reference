### Differential diagnosis
- Wide-open fellow angle → not PAC — NAMRATA fitz 199
- Plateau iris syndrome: deep central and shallow peripheral AC, flat iris — ARAVIND fitz 231 (Q8); double hump — BAIDYA fitz 166; occludable despite patent PI — BAIDYA fitz 166, ARAVIND fitz 231, NAMRATA fitz 199; younger — BAIDYA fitz 166, ARAVIND fitz 230 (Q7)
- Lens-induced closure (intumescent/swollen or subluxated lens) — BAIDYA fitz 165 (Q1a); NAMRATA fitz 199, 201 (Q3)
- Malignant glaucoma: intraocular surgery, diffuse shallowing, normal or raised IOP, UBM anterior rotation of ciliary processes — BAIDYA fitz 165 (Q1b)
- Neovascular glaucoma: new vessels on iris or angle the hallmark — NAMRATA fitz 199

### Definition and classification
- Definition (appositional or synechial closure by pupillary block, ± IOP or optic neuropathy, acute or chronic, excludes secondary) — NAMRATA fitz 196
- ISGEO based on IOP, gonioscopy, disc and fields — NAMRATA fitz 202 (Q8)
- PACS: ITC ≥ 3 quadrants (almost 270°), no PAS, normal IOP, disc and field — BAIDYA fitz 165; ARAVIND fitz 229 (> 270°)
- PAC: ITC + raised IOP and/or PAS; normal disc and fields — ARAVIND fitz 229; NAMRATA fitz 200 (Table 1 footnote *); BAIDYA fitz 165 (PAS, normal disc and field)
- PACG: + glaucomatous optic neuropathy (cupping with field loss) — BAIDYA fitz 165; NAMRATA fitz 200 (footnote **); ARAVIND fitz 229
- Four sites of closure: iris (pupillary block), ciliary body (plateau iris), lens (phacomorphic), behind the iris (malignant and other posterior pushing) — ARAVIND fitz 227 (4.5)
- Lens mechanisms: thick intumescent lens, subluxated lens — NAMRATA fitz 201 (Q3)
- Posterior "pushing" (ciliary block, cysts) and anterior "pulling" (NVG, ICE) — ARAVIND fitz 229 (Q1)
- Shaffer 4 = 35–45°, 3 = 25–35°, 2 = 10–25°, 1 = 0–10°, 0 = 0° — TYPED FORMAT fitz 1
- Spaeth: angular width 10–50°, iris configuration S/R/Q, iris-root insertion A–E — TYPED FORMAT fitz 1
- Shaffer, Spaeth, Scheie named as gonioscopy grading systems — BAIDYA fitz 164; NAMRATA fitz 199

### Pathogenesis
- Relative pupillary block: lens–iris touch, aqueous accumulates in posterior chamber, iris bows forward — BAIDYA fitz 165 (Q3)

### Investigations
- AS-OCT: non-contact, quick, 360°, lens vault; cannot penetrate behind the iris pigment epithelium — IMAGING fitz 22
- AS-OCT shows an occludable angle — BAIDYA fitz 165 (PACS column)
- AS-OCT may be superior to gonioscopy in detecting occludability — NAMRATA fitz 200
- UBM 50–100 MHz; axial resolution 25 µm; penetration about 4–5 mm — ARAVIND fitz 227

- UBM images through corneal oedema or opacity; bulky — ARAVIND fitz 228
- UBM measures AC depth (also corneal thickness, posterior chamber depth, IOL and scleral thickness) — ARAVIND fitz 228 (Q4)
- UBM: anterior iris bowing (pupillary block); double hump on indentation UBM (plateau iris); everything pressed against the cornea ± supraciliary fluid (malignant) — ARAVIND fitz 227
- UBM identifies plateau iris and iridociliary cysts — NAMRATA fitz 200
- Echogenic / hypoechoic / echolucent (UBM); hyperreflective / hyporeflective (OCT); radial and transverse scans — IMAGING fitz 22
- Automated perimetry — NAMRATA fitz 200
- CCT postponed until an acute attack resolves — NAMRATA fitz 198; CCT influences applanation readings — NAMRATA fitz 196
- Provocative tests listed — ARAVIND fitz 234 (Q26); mydriatic (0.5% tropicamide; pupil 4–6 mm) and dark room (90 minutes, awake) positive at IOP rise > 8 mm Hg or closure on gonioscopy — ARAVIND fitz 234 (page image); prone 45–60 minutes, Perkins tonometer — ARAVIND fitz 235 (page image)

- Pilocarpine 1%; proparacaine 0.5% — NAMRATA fitz 201
- Brimonidine or apraclonidine half an hour before — ARAVIND fitz 294
- Abraham lens +55 D button: stabilises, magnifies, increases power density — NAMRATA fitz 201
- Site: peripheral third, crypt, 11–1 o'clock under the lid — NAMRATA fitz 201
- Upper nasal iris avoids diplopia and macular burns (viva) — ARAVIND fitz 294
- 4–8 mJ, 1–3 pulses per burst, fixed spot — NAMRATA fitz 202
- Size at least 200 µm, preferably 500 µm — NAMRATA fitz 201
- End point: gush of aqueous or pigment from posterior to anterior chamber, sudden deepening — NAMRATA fitz 201–202; ARAVIND fitz 294 (Q10)
- IOP at 1 hour; significant rise = ≥ 8 mm Hg or IOP > 30 mm Hg — NAMRATA fitz 202
- Prednisolone acetate 1% four times a day for 5–7 days — NAMRATA fitz 202
- Gonioscopy at 1 week; lens capsule must be seen through the iridotomy — ARAVIND fitz 294
- Argon: thermal, needs pigment; for chronic anticoagulant therapy — ARAVIND fitz 293 (Q6, Q7)
- LPI contraindications: corneal oedema, flat AC, completely sealed / synechial angle, uveitis, NVG — ARAVIND fitz 293 (Q5)
- Acute attack (ARAVIND fitz 238, 4.7): mannitol 20% IV 1–2 g/kg over 20–30 minutes; miotics fail at very high IOP; acetazolamide 250 mg four times a day if no nausea or vomiting; glycerol 50% 1–1.5 g/kg after ruling out diabetes; apraclonidine 0.5%; pilocarpine 2% four times over 30 minutes then 6-hourly once IOP < 40 mm Hg; timolol 0.5% twice daily + brimonidine 0.2% three times daily; prednisolone acetate 1% four times a day; ibuprofen 500 mg twice daily; hourly review; gonioscopy of both eyes when cornea clear; central corneal depression may open the angle; Nd:YAG PI both eyes when quiet and clear; surgical iridectomy if cornea hazy and closure < 2/3 (YAG for fellow eye); primary trabeculectomy if closure > 2/3 or persistent inflammation with oedema and high IOP
- Acetazolamide 250–500 mg orally stat — NAMRATA fitz 200
- Antiemetic — NAMRATA fitz 200
- Plateau iris: PI first; argon laser iridoplasty if confirmed — ARAVIND fitz 231 (Q10)
- Iridoplasty 200–400 mW, 0.1 second, 20–24 spots over 360° — ARAVIND fitz 297 (Q25)
- Medical after LPI as for POAG; beta-blocker or continue drops — NAMRATA fitz 200 (Table 1), 202

## Disagreements (card uses the newer book unless stated)
1. ISGEO contact threshold: BAIDYA fitz 165 "≥ 3 quadrants (almost 270°)"; ARAVIND fitz 229 "> 270°"; NAMRATA fitz 199–200 "> 180°". Card: Baidya (≥ 3 quadrants, about 270°). The candidate should know the > 180° version exists.
2. PAC criteria: BAIDYA lists "ITC or PAS in ≥ 3 quadrants, normal disc and field, shallow AC" with no IOP criterion; ARAVIND and NAMRATA say "raised IOP and/or PAS". Card: raised IOP and/or PAS (Baidya is silent rather than contrary).
3. PACG criteria: BAIDYA "ITC ≥ 3 quadrants; IOP raised, glaucomatous cupping with field loss — any of the above"; ARAVIND "elevated IOP plus optic nerve and field damage"; NAMRATA "raised IOP or PAS + ITC > 180° + optic nerve damage". Card: "raised IOP and/or PAS + glaucomatous optic neuropathy (cupping, field loss)".
4. Vogt's triad: BAIDYA fitz 164 and NAMRATA fitz 198 = glaukomflecken + sectoral iris atrophy + Krukenberg spindle; ARAVIND fitz 236 (Q31) = glaukomflecken + patches of iris atrophy + slightly dilated non-reacting pupil. Card: Baidya. An examiner trained on Aravind may expect the pupil version.
5. Sphincter ischaemia threshold: BAIDYA fitz 166 > 40 mm Hg; ARAVIND fitz 238 > 50 mm Hg (miotics fail). Card: 40 mm Hg, which also matches Aravind's "start pilocarpine once IOP is below 40".
6. Mannitol: ARAVIND fitz 238 1–2 g/kg over 20–30 minutes; NAMRATA fitz 200 1.5 g/kg of 20% (200 mL) over 30 minutes. Card: Aravind, as the brief asked for Aravind 4.7 doses. Namrata's figure lies inside Aravind's range.
7. Acetazolamide: ARAVIND 250 mg four times a day (no loading dose); NAMRATA 250–500 mg stat, then 125–250 mg three or four times a day (or 250 mg four times a day after mannitol). Card: 250–500 mg stat (Namrata), then 250 mg four times a day (both).
8. Pilocarpine in the attack: ARAVIND 2% four times in 30 minutes then 6-hourly, once IOP < 40; NAMRATA 2% stat then four times a day, after inflammation is controlled. Card: Aravind (brief).
9. Pre-LPI pilocarpine: NAMRATA fitz 201 1%; ARAVIND fitz 294 2% three times, 5 minutes apart. Card: 1%.
10. Abraham lens button: NAMRATA +55 D; ARAVIND +66 D. Card: +55 D.
11. LPI energy: NAMRATA 4–8 mJ; ARAVIND 3–8 mJ. Card: 4–8 mJ.

13. LPI site: NAMRATA 11–1 o'clock under the lid; ARAVIND upper nasal iris (diplopia, macular burns). Compatible; both used.
