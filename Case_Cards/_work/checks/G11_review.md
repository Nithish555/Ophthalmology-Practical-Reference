4. **Management with triggers and essentials.** Mannitol rate added (CONSISTENCY 20); apraclonidine mechanism added; CAI contraindications in full on buphthalmos and pigmentary (CONSISTENCY 17). Procedures now carry only book-supported advantages and disadvantages: trabeculotomy ("can be done with a hazy cornea"), Sturge–Weber trabeculectomy (outflow independent of the episcleral veins), drainage devices and cyclodestruction (their complications), uveitic Ahmed valve (needs steroid cover), angle-recession drainage devices (limited benefit). The inferred Nd:YAG "advantage/disadvantage" pair was removed and replaced by the books' mechanism.
5. **Pigmentary age** set to 30–50 years (CONSISTENCY 28); pseudoexfoliation row "over 60" to match G6.
