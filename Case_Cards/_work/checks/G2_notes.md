### Definition and classification
- Partial-thickness operation; fistula at the limbus; aqueous around the flap edges into the subconjunctival space; transconjunctival filtration or lymphatics and vessels; works mainly by filtration — ARAVIND fitz 301–302 (Q3, Q6)
- Failing vs failed: failing = Q9 signs; failed = does not filter (types 3, 4) — ARAVIND fitz 302 (see Format decisions: the packet has no formal definition)
- Filtering operations: full thickness (sclerectomy, trephination, Scheie's thermal sclerostomy, laser sclerostomy, iridencleisis, goniopuncture); partial thickness (trabeculectomy); non-penetrating (viscocanalostomy, canaloplasty, deep sclerectomy) — ARAVIND fitz 301 (Q1)
- Four bleb types: 1 thin polycystic; 2 flat, thin, diffuse, relatively avascular, microcysts on high magnification; 3 flat, no microcysts, engorged vessels, does not filter; 4 encapsulated / Tenon's cyst, localised, highly elevated, dome-shaped cavity of hypertrophied Tenon's capsule, engorged vessels, does not filter — ARAVIND fitz 302 (Q10)

## Disagreements

- **Failure-management bullets**: Q42's single list was split by stage.
