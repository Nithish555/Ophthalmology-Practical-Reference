### Differential diagnosis
- Primary acute angle closure: no rubeosis of iris and angle, no fibrovascular membrane, no ectropion uveae — ARAVIND fitz 249 (Q18 i); NAMRATA fitz 212 (acute-onset glaucoma in the open-angle stage)
- Uveitic glaucoma: KPs, flare, cells, posterior synechiae — ARAVIND fitz 249 (Q18 ii); uveitic eyes may have dilated iris vessels, NVG has true rubeosis — NAMRATA fitz 212
- Fuchs' heterochromic iridocyclitis: white quiet eye, angle vessels (filiform), NVI and NVG rare, spontaneous hyphaema, stellate KPs, heterochromia — ARAVIND fitz 250 (Q19)
- ICE syndrome: corneal decompensation, corectopia, pseudopolycoria, iris atrophy — ARAVIND fitz 249 (Q18 iv); iris distortion and PAS in the angle-closure stage — NAMRATA fitz 212
- Old trauma: angle recession, pigment clumps, no NVI — ARAVIND fitz 249 (Q18 v); post-traumatic hyphaema strongly associated with angle recession (60–90%) — NAMRATA fitz 215
- Normal iris vessels: stromal vs superficial; radial vs irregular; uniform vs varying size; no branching; new vessels leak on fluorescein angiography/fluorophotometry — NAMRATA fitz 212, 214 (Q4); BAIDYA fitz 192; ARAVIND fitz 247 (Q6)

## Disagreements (and what the card uses)
1. **Prednisolone acetate 1% frequency** — BAIDYA fitz 193: 6–8 times/day; ARAVIND fitz 253: four times daily. Card: Baidya (6–8 times a day).
2. **Cycloplegic** — BAIDYA fitz 193: atropine 1% (or homatropine 2%) three times daily; ARAVIND fitz 253: "atropine eye ointment 4%". Card: Baidya.
3. **Anti-VEGF timing before surgery** — BAIDYA fitz 194: 2–3 days before; NAMRATA fitz 212: usually 1 week before, can be 14–48 hours; ARAVIND fitz 254: 24–78 hours before. Card: Baidya (2–3 days).
4. **Prostaglandin analogues** — BAIDYA fitz 193: contraindicated; NAMRATA fitz 212: better avoided; ARAVIND fitz 250: relative contraindication. Card: "avoid" / contraindicated (Baidya); "relative" not used.
5. **Mechanism of ectropion uveae** — BAIDYA fitz 192: "due to repeated uveitic attack as a part of NVI"; ARAVIND fitz 246 (Q4) and NAMRATA fitz 214: traction/contraction of the fibrovascular membrane. Card: membrane traction (two books agree; mechanism, not a number or treatment). Flag for reviewer.
6. **IOP in the angle-closure stage** — NAMRATA fitz 211 table: "persistently high (> 60 mm)"; NAMRATA fitz 214 text: "can go up to 60 mm Hg". Same book. Card: "persistently high, up to 60 mm Hg".

9. **Timing of NVG after CRVO** — NAMRATA fitz 214: "after 3 months"; ARAVIND fitz 249: usually 3–5 months, range 2 months–2 years. Compatible; card uses both.
10. **NVI regression after PRP** — BAIDYA fitz 194: "within a few weeks"; NAMRATA fitz 212: starts by 2 weeks, complete by 4–6 weeks. Compatible; card uses Namrata's numbers.
11. **Goniophotocoagulation** — BAIDYA fitz 194 places it under "poor or no visual prognosis" yet says "may be tried in eyes with good prognosis"; ARAVIND fitz 251: inadequate alone, adjunct to PRP. Not on the card (cut for length).
